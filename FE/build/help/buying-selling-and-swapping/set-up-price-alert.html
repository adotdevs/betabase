How to set up a price alert

A price alert sends you a push or email notification when an asset crosses a price you choose. It does not place an order — nothing is bought or sold.

Steps

  1. Open the asset's page in the Betabase app or on www.betabase.pro.
  2. Select the bell icon, or More → Set price alert.
  3. Choose the direction: Rises above or Falls below.
  4. Enter the price in your display currency.
  5. Choose the delivery channel — push notification and/or email.
  6. Select Save. The alert appears under Settings → Notifications → Price alerts.

Good to know

  • Push notifications require notifications to be enabled for the Betabase app in your phone's system settings. This is the most common reason an alert appears not to fire.
  • Alerts are one-shot by default: once triggered, the alert is marked as used. Re-arm it if you want it again.
  • Alerts are based on Betabase's reference price and are informational only. There may be a short delay between the price being reached and the notification arriving.
  • You can set several alerts per asset, up to the limit shown on the alerts screen.
  • To act automatically instead of manually, use a limit or stop order rather than an alert.

An alert is not an order and gives you no guarantee that the price will still be there when you open the app. Crypto is volatile and your capital is at risk.
