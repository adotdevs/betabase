How to swap one crypto for another

A swap exchanges one crypto directly for another in a single order, without you selling to EUR and buying again.

Steps

  1. Select Trade → Swap (called Convert in some regions).
  2. In the From field, choose the asset you hold and the amount to swap.
  3. In the To field, choose the asset you want to receive.
  4. Review the quote: the estimated quantity you receive, the implied rate, the spread and any fee.
  5. Select Confirm before the quote timer expires.

Good to know

  • Not every pair is swappable. If a direct pair isn't offered, sell to EUR and buy the second asset in two orders.
  • Swaps are usually executed as market orders — you cannot set a limit price on a swap.
  • A swap is economically two trades in one, so the cost is roughly the combined spread of both sides. Compare the quote against selling and buying separately if the amount is large.
  • Both legs settle at the same moment, so you are never left holding only half of the swap.
  • In most jurisdictions a crypto-to-crypto swap is a disposal and therefore a taxable event, even though no EUR touched your account.

Crypto is volatile and your capital is at risk. A swap does not reduce risk — it simply moves it from one volatile asset to another.
