How long do withdrawals take?

Crypto withdrawals are usually broadcast within minutes and confirm shortly after. Bank withdrawals typically arrive in 1–2 business days for SEPA and 2–5 for international wires.

Typical timings

Withdrawal typeTypical time
CryptoBroadcast in minutes; confirmations then depend on the network
SEPA transfer1–2 business days
International wire (SWIFT)2–5 business days
Any withdrawal under security holdAdd 24–72 hours
Any withdrawal under compliance reviewAdd 1–3 business days

What makes it slower

  • A security setting changed in the last 72 hours.
  • A brand-new bank account or wallet address, still inside its cooling-off period.
  • A larger-than-usual amount, triggering a review.
  • Bank cut-off times, weekends and public holidays — bank rails do not run then. Crypto networks run 24/7.
  • Network congestion, once the crypto transaction is already broadcast.

Good to know

  • Once a crypto withdrawal shows a TXID, Betabase's part is finished. The rest is the blockchain and the receiving platform, whose own confirmation requirements you cannot change.
  • The receiving exchange or wallet may add its own delay before crediting you.
  • If a fiat withdrawal has not arrived after 5 business days, ask your bank to trace it first, then contact support@www.betabase.pro with the date and amount.

Blockchain transactions are irreversible. A broadcast withdrawal cannot be cancelled or recalled, however long it takes to confirm.
