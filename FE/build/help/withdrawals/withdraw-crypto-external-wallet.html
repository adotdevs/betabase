How to withdraw cryptocurrency to an external wallet

Withdraw crypto by selecting the asset, pasting the destination address, choosing the correct network, and confirming with 2FA — then the transaction goes on-chain and cannot be undone.

Before you start

  • Have the destination address copied from the receiving wallet, and know exactly which network that wallet expects.
  • Check whether the destination needs a memo / destination tag.
  • Check the withdrawal minimum for that asset and the network fee.

Steps

  1. Select Withdraw → Crypto and choose the asset.
  2. Paste the destination address using Paste or scan the receiving wallet's QR code. Never retype an address by hand.
  3. Choose the network. It must match what the receiving wallet supports.
  4. If a memo/destination tag is required, enter it in the memo field — not in the address field.
  5. Enter the amount, or select Max. The network fee and the net amount that will actually arrive are shown.
  6. Verify the address: compare the first four and last four characters against the original. Clipboard-hijacking malware silently swaps addresses.
  7. Select Confirm and complete 2FA, plus any email confirmation link.
  8. Once broadcast, you get a transaction hash (TXID) in Transactions. Paste it into a block explorer to follow its confirmations.

Good to know

  • Send a small test amount first to any new address. The test fee is trivial compared with the loss.
  • The receiving platform decides how many confirmations it needs before crediting you. Betabase's job ends when the transaction is broadcast.
  • Whitelisting an address makes future withdrawals to it faster and much safer.

Blockchain transactions are irreversible. Once broadcast and confirmed, a transfer cannot be cancelled, reversed or recalled — not by you, not by Betabase, not by anyone. Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys.
