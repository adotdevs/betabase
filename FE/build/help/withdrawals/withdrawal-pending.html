Why is my withdrawal pending?

Pending means the withdrawal has been accepted but not yet released — usually because of a routine security hold, a compliance check, or unsettled funds.

The common causes

  • Security hold. You recently changed your password, email or 2FA, or added a new address or bank account. Withdrawals are held for 24–72 hours after these changes.
  • Unsettled funds. Money from a recent card or bank deposit may need to settle before it can leave again.
  • Compliance review. Larger or unusual withdrawals go to a specialist. This is routine, not an accusation, and typically clears within a business day or two.
  • Tax Security Deposit. In some cases a temporary, separate security amount may be required to complete tax-related verification before funds can be released. See Tax Security Deposit – Frequently Asked Questions.
  • Travel Rule information. We may need to know who owns the receiving wallet before we can release a transfer.
  • Network congestion. For crypto, once broadcast the transaction is out of our hands and simply needs confirmations.
  • Bank cut-off times. Fiat withdrawals submitted after the daily cut-off, or at a weekend, process on the next business day.

Steps

  1. Open Transactions and select the withdrawal. The status note usually names the reason.
  2. Check your email — a pending withdrawal often just needs a confirmation link clicked.
  3. Check whether you changed a security setting in the last 72 hours.
  4. If the status shows a TXID, the transfer is already on-chain. Track it on a block explorer — it is confirming, not stuck with us.
  5. Still unexplained after 3 business days? Contact support@www.betabase.pro with the transaction reference. Support is available 24/7.

Good to know

  • Cancelling and resubmitting rarely helps and often restarts the clock.
  • A pending withdrawal has already been deducted from your available balance. That is expected — the funds are reserved, not lost.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys, and will never ask you to pay a fee to "release" a pending withdrawal. Anyone who does is a scammer.
