The 24–72h security hold after changing your password or 2FA

After certain security changes, Betabase holds all withdrawals for 24–72 hours. This delay exists to stop an attacker who has just taken over an account from emptying it.

What triggers a hold

  • Changing your password.
  • Adding, changing, resetting or removing 2FA.
  • Changing your registered email address or phone number.
  • Adding a new withdrawal address or a new bank account.
  • Signing in from a new device or an unusual location, in some cases.

Why we cannot lift it early

The hold is the security control, not an administrative step. Account takeovers follow a fixed pattern: the attacker gets in, changes the password and 2FA to lock you out, then withdraws everything within minutes. The hold breaks that chain — it gives you a window to see the alert email, regain control and freeze the account before anything leaves. If support could waive it on request, a scammer impersonating you could ask for exactly that. No employee can shorten or remove it, no matter who asks or why.

Steps if you did not make the change

  1. Sign in immediately and change your password.
  2. Go to Settings → Security → Devices and revoke every session you do not recognise.
  3. Reset 2FA.
  4. Use Freeze my account to block all activity at once.
  5. Contact support@www.betabase.pro straight away. Support is available 24/7.

Good to know

  • Trading is not affected — you can still buy and sell during a hold. Only withdrawals wait.
  • The clock starts at the moment of the change, not at the moment you request a withdrawal.
  • Plan around it: if you know you need to withdraw, do it before you rotate your security settings.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys, and will never offer to bypass the hold.
