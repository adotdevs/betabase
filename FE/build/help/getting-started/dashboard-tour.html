A quick tour of your dashboard

Your dashboard is the home screen of Betabase: it shows your total portfolio value, each asset you hold, and the buttons to deposit, trade and withdraw.

What you see

  1. Portfolio value — the combined value of your cash and crypto, shown in your chosen display currency. This updates continuously with market prices.
  2. Asset list — each coin or token you hold, its quantity, its current value and its change over your selected time range.
  3. Cash balance — your uninvested EUR held with Betabase. Only cash can be used to buy.
  4. Deposit / Withdraw — add funds or take them out. These open the funding flows.
  5. Trade / Buy / Sell / Swap — open an order ticket for any supported asset.
  6. Transactions — the full, filterable history of every deposit, trade, withdrawal, fee and reward. This is where you export statements.
  7. Profile & Settings — personal details, verification status, security, limits, language and display currency.

Good to know

  • Percentage changes are shown against the period you select, not against your purchase price. Your personal profit or loss appears on the individual asset page.
  • Prices are indicative until an order is executed. The price you get is the price at execution.
  • Portfolio value can go down as well as up. Crypto is volatile and your capital is at risk.
