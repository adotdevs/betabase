How to create a Betabase account

You create a Betabase account by registering with your email address on www.betabase.pro or in the Betabase app, confirming that email, and setting a strong password.

Before you start

  • A valid email address you control and can open right now.
  • A mobile phone that can receive SMS or run an authenticator app.
  • You must meet the sign-up requirements (see What you need to sign up).

Steps

  1. Go to www.betabase.pro and select Sign up, or open the Betabase app and tap Get started.
  2. Enter your email address and choose your country of residence.
  3. Create a password. Use at least 12 characters mixing upper case, lower case, numbers and symbols.
  4. Tick the boxes to accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  5. Select Create account. We send a confirmation link to your email.
  6. Open the email and select Confirm my email. The link is single-use and expires after a short time.
  7. Sign in with your new credentials and follow the on-screen prompt to set up two-factor authentication (2FA).

Good to know

  • One person may hold one account. Duplicate accounts are closed on detection.
  • Business accounts follow a separate application process.
  • No confirmation email? Check spam, then use Resend email. Add our sending domain to your safe-sender list.
  • Creating an account is free. You can browse markets before you verify, but you cannot deposit or trade until verification is complete.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Only ever type your password into www.betabase.pro or the Betabase app.
