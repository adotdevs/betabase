How to download and set up the Betabase app

Download the Betabase app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, sign in with your existing Betabase credentials, and enable biometric unlock.

Before you start

  • Only download from the official App Store or Google Play listing linked from www.betabase.pro. Fake crypto apps are common.
  • Check the publisher name and the review count before you install.

Steps

  1. Open the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) and search for the Betabase app, or use the download links on www.betabase.pro.
  2. Install the app and open it.
  3. Tap Log in and enter the email and password you use on the web.
  4. Complete your 2FA challenge.
  5. When prompted, enable Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint unlock so you don't type your password on a screen someone could watch.
  6. Set a 6-digit app PIN as a fallback.
  7. Allow push notifications so you receive login alerts, order fills and deposit confirmations.

Good to know

  • Your web and app accounts are the same account. Balances, orders and verification status are shared instantly.
  • Biometric unlock replaces your password on that device only. It does not replace 2FA for sensitive actions such as withdrawals.
  • If you lose the phone, sign in on the web and revoke the device under Settings → Security → Devices.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Never install a "support tool" or screen-sharing app at the request of someone claiming to be from Betabase.
