How to buy your first cryptocurrency

Once you have a cash balance, select an asset, enter how much EUR you want to spend, review the quote and confirm the order.

Before you start

  • Verification complete and a settled cash balance on your dashboard.
  • Read Is crypto risky? first. Only commit money you can afford to lose.

Steps

  1. From the dashboard, select Trade (or Buy).
  2. Search for the asset you want and select it.
  3. Choose Buy.
  4. Enter an amount in EUR — you do not need to buy a whole coin, fractions are fine.
  5. Choose the order type. A market order executes immediately at the best available price. A limit order only executes at your chosen price or better.
  6. Review the summary: the amount of crypto you receive, the price, and the fee.
  7. Select Confirm. The order appears instantly under Transactions.

Good to know

  • Quotes are time-limited. If the quote expires, refresh it — the market has moved.
  • The purchased crypto shows in your portfolio as soon as the order fills.
  • A small spread and/or fee applies to each trade. It is shown before you confirm, never after.
  • Crypto prices are volatile. The value of your holding can fall sharply and you may get back less than you put in. Nothing in this help centre is investment advice.
