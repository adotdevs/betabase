What is staking and how does it work on Betabase?

Staking means committing crypto to help secure a proof-of-stake blockchain. In return, the network may pay rewards in that same asset. On Betabase, eligible assets can be staked from your portfolio without you running a validator yourself.

How it works

  1. A proof-of-stake network needs participants to lock up coins as collateral and validate transactions.
  2. The network issues rewards to those validators, and penalises dishonest or offline ones.
  3. Betabase pools eligible balances and stakes them with validators on your behalf. Any reward received, less any service fee disclosed in the fee schedule, is credited to you.

Steps

  1. Open the asset in Portfolio. If staking is offered in your country, you'll see a Stake option.
  2. Read the product terms in full, including the lock-up and unstaking periods.
  3. Enter the amount to stake and confirm.
  4. The staked balance is shown separately in your portfolio. Rewards appear under Transactions as they are received.

Good to know

  • Rewards are variable. They depend on network conditions, validator performance and the total amount staked network-wide. They are not interest, not a deposit and not a fixed yield.
  • Staked assets are usually locked and cannot be sold, swapped or withdrawn until unstaked.
  • Not all assets or all countries support staking.

Your capital is at risk. Staking does not protect the value of your holding — the asset's price can fall by more than any reward. Rewards are variable and never guaranteed, and in some cases can be zero. This is not investment advice.
