What is a savings plan (recurring buy / DCA)?

A savings plan is a standing instruction to buy a fixed amount of an asset on a repeating schedule — weekly, fortnightly or monthly — without you placing each order by hand.

How it works

  1. You choose an asset, an instalment amount in EUR, a frequency and a funding source.
  2. On each execution date, Betabase places a buy order for that amount automatically.
  3. The crypto lands in your portfolio and the plan waits for the next date.

Cost averaging, explained honestly

Because the instalment is a fixed EUR amount, you automatically buy a smaller quantity when the price is high and a larger quantity when it is low. Over many instalments this produces an average entry price rather than a single one, which removes the need to pick a moment to buy.

That is the mechanic — and that is all it is. It is not a strategy that produces profit, and it does not protect you from loss. If the asset's price falls over the life of your plan, cost averaging simply means you bought the fall in pieces. You can still lose money, including all of it.

Good to know

  • Each instalment is a normal trade and is subject to the usual spread and fee.
  • Instalments have their own minimum, which may differ from the one-off trade minimum.
  • You can run several plans across different assets at once.

A savings plan does not guarantee any return, and it is not investment advice. Crypto is volatile and your capital is at risk — you may get back less than you put in.
