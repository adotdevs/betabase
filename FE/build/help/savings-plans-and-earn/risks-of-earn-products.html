Risks of Earn products (capital at risk, no guaranteed yield)

Earn and staking products are not savings accounts. They pay variable rewards in a volatile asset, they lock your funds, and you can lose money — including everything you put in.

The risks in full

  • Price risk — the big one. Rewards are paid in the asset you staked. If that asset's price falls, the value of your holding falls with it, and any reward can be wiped out many times over. A positive reward rate and a negative outcome are entirely compatible.
  • No guaranteed yield. Rates are set by the network and vary continuously. They can fall, and in some conditions they can be zero. Any rate shown anywhere on Betabase is an estimate based on recent conditions, never a promise or an offer.
  • Lock-up and illiquidity. Staked assets cannot be sold, swapped or withdrawn until unstaked, and unbonding takes time. You may be unable to exit precisely when you most want to.
  • Slashing. Proof-of-stake networks penalise validators that misbehave or go offline by destroying part of the stake. Where a network applies slashing, some or all of a staked balance can be lost.
  • Protocol and technical risk. Bugs, network upgrades, forks and halts can affect rewards or access to assets.
  • No protection scheme. Crypto held on any platform is generally not covered by deposit-guarantee or investor-compensation schemes the way bank cash is.
  • Regulatory and tax risk. Availability can change, and rewards are usually taxable income in your country.

Your capital is at risk. You may lose some or all of it. Earn products are not deposits, not savings, not interest and not investment advice. Never commit money you cannot afford to lose, and be deeply sceptical of any product — anywhere — that advertises a guaranteed or fixed crypto return. Guaranteed returns are a hallmark of fraud.
