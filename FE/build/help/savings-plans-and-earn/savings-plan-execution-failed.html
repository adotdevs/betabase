Why a savings plan execution failed

A skipped instalment almost always comes down to money, limits or the asset itself. The exact reason is shown on the plan's detail page.

The common causes

  • Insufficient cash balance. For plans funded from your Betabase balance, the full instalment plus fee must be available on the execution date. Reserved funds on open orders do not count.
  • The direct debit or card payment was declined. Your bank returned it — insufficient funds, an expired card, a cancelled mandate, or the bank's own fraud block. Fix it with your bank first, then update the funding source on Betabase.
  • A limit was reached. A daily or monthly limit, or your verification tier's cap, blocked the buy. Check Profile → Limits.
  • The asset is suspended. Trading may be paused for a network upgrade, a listing review or extreme volatility. The instalment is skipped for that asset only.
  • Verification lapsed. An expired ID document puts the account into a restricted state, which stops plans.

What happens next

  1. You are notified by email and in-app.
  2. The plan is not deleted. It stays active and tries again on the next scheduled date.
  3. Missed instalments are not automatically caught up. If you want the missed amount invested, place a one-off buy manually.
  4. After several consecutive failures the plan may be paused automatically. Resume it under Savings plans → Manage once the cause is fixed.
