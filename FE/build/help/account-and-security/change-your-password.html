How to change your password

Change your password in Settings → Security → Password, or use Forgot password? on the login screen if you cannot sign in.

Before you start

  • Have access to your 2FA device — you will be asked for a code.
  • Choose a password you use nowhere else. A password manager is the easiest way to do this.

Steps

  1. Sign in and open Profile → Settings → Security.
  2. Select Change password.
  3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
  4. Enter your 2FA code to confirm.
  5. Select Save. You are signed out of all other devices.

If you have forgotten it

  1. On the login screen select Forgot password?.
  2. Enter your registered email. We send a reset link — it is single-use and expires shortly.
  3. Open the link, set a new password, and complete your 2FA challenge.

Good to know

  • A password change is a security-sensitive event. For your protection, withdrawals may be paused for up to 24 hours afterwards.
  • If you did not request a password reset, ignore the email and do not click the link — then change your password and review your active sessions.
  • We never email you your password, because we cannot read it.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Only ever enter your password on www.betabase.pro or in the Betabase app.
