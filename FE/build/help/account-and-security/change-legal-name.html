How to change your legal name

You can change your legal name by contacting support with an official document evidencing the change. It cannot be edited directly in settings.

Before you start

Have a clear colour scan of both:

  • your updated photo ID showing the new name (passport or national ID card); and
  • the document evidencing the change — marriage certificate, divorce decree, deed poll, or a court/registry order.

Steps

  1. Sign in and open Help → Contact support, or email support@www.betabase.pro from your registered address.
  2. Choose the topic Update personal details → Legal name.
  3. State your old name and your new name exactly as they appear on the documents.
  4. Attach both documents.
  5. Submit. You will get a ticket reference.

Good to know

  • A simple typo (a misspelling that was never correct) also goes through this process, but only needs your ID.
  • Review typically takes 2–5 business days because it is a manual compliance check.
  • Your name must match the name on the bank account you fund from. If they diverge, deposits will be returned — so update your bank and Betabase.
  • Withdrawals may be paused while the change is processed.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Send documents only through the in-app support form or support@www.betabase.pro.
