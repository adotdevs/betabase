How to change your phone number

Update your mobile number in Settings → Personal details → Phone number. You verify the new number with an SMS code.

Before you start

  • Have the new SIM active and in a phone that can receive SMS.
  • If SMS is your 2FA method, changing the number also changes where your codes go — do this before you lose the old number if you can.

Steps

  1. Open Profile → Settings → Personal details.
  2. Next to Phone number, select Change.
  3. Enter your password and complete your 2FA challenge.
  4. Choose the correct country code and enter the new number without a leading zero.
  5. Select Send code. Enter the 6-digit SMS code within the time limit.
  6. Select Confirm.

If you no longer have the old number

  • If you use an authenticator app for 2FA, you can still complete the steps above normally.
  • If you used SMS 2FA on the old number, you are locked out of 2FA. Follow How to reset 2FA when you lose your device — this needs an identity check and takes longer.

Good to know

  • SMS delivery depends on your mobile carrier. Delays of a few minutes happen; roaming can block delivery entirely.
  • Withdrawals may be paused for a short cooling-off period after the change.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. SIM-swap fraud is real — an authenticator app is safer than SMS.
