How to close your account permanently

Close your account in Settings → Account → Close account. You must sell or withdraw all balances first, and closure is permanent.

Before you start

  • Sell or withdraw every asset. An account cannot be closed with a non-zero balance, and small "dust" amounts may need to be swapped or sold first.
  • Export your statements and transaction history. You will need them for tax reporting, and you will not be able to sign in afterwards.
  • Cancel any active savings plans or recurring buys.

Steps

  1. Open Profile → Settings → Account.
  2. Select Close account.
  3. Confirm that your balances are zero. The screen lists anything still outstanding.
  4. Enter your password and your 2FA code.
  5. Choose a reason (optional) and select Close permanently.
  6. Confirm the link sent to your registered email.

Good to know

  • Closure is irreversible. To return you must register from scratch and verify again.
  • We are legally required to retain certain records — identity documents, transaction history — for a period set by anti-money-laundering law, typically several years. This applies even if you request deletion of your personal data.
  • Any funds arriving after closure will be returned to the sender where possible; on-chain deposits sent to a closed account address may be permanently lost.
  • If you simply want a break, freeze the account instead — that is reversible.
