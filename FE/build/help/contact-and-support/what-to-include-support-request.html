What information to include in your support request (so we solve it first time)

The single biggest cause of slow support is a missing detail. Send everything below in your first message and you avoid a round-trip that can add days.

What to include

  1. What you were trying to do, in one sentence. ("Withdraw 0.05 BTC to my hardware wallet.")
  2. What actually happened, including the exact error message. Copy the text or screenshot it — "it didn't work" tells us nothing.
  3. The transaction ID or reference for anything involving money. This is the single most useful thing you can give us. Find it in Transactions → select the item → Details.
  4. Dates and times, with your time zone.
  5. Amounts and assets, exactly.
  6. Where it happened: web or the Betabase app; your browser or OS version; your device.
  7. What you already tried, so we don't ask you to repeat it.
  8. Screenshots. Show the whole screen, including the address bar and any error, not a cropped fragment.

Never include

  • Your password, your 2FA code, a seed phrase or a private key. We will never ask for these and a genuine agent will never accept them.
  • Full card numbers or your CVV.

Good to know

  • Use the email address registered to your account. A message from an unknown address triggers extra identity checks before we can discuss anything.
  • One issue per ticket. Bundling several unrelated problems into one thread slows all of them down.
  • Keep replying on the same thread rather than opening a new ticket — a new ticket goes to the back of the queue.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys.
