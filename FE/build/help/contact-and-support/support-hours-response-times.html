Support hours and expected response times

Our support hours are 24/7. Live chat is answered within those hours; tickets and emails are answered in the order received, with security issues prioritised.

Typical response times

  • Live chat: usually within minutes during 24/7.
  • Support ticket / email: usually within one to two business days.
  • Security issues (suspected compromise, unauthorised transactions): prioritised and handled ahead of the general queue.
  • Cases needing document review (verification, source of funds, complaints): longer, because they involve manual review by a specialist team.

What makes it slower

  • Missing information — the most common cause. See What information to include in your support request.
  • Replying from an address not registered to the account.
  • Opening a new ticket instead of replying to the existing one. It resets your place in the queue.
  • Weekends, public holidays, and periods of extreme market volatility, when volumes spike.

Good to know

  • We work in the order tickets arrive. Chasing a ticket adds a message to the thread but does not move it up the queue.
  • Many of the most common questions — a pending withdrawal, a delayed deposit, an SMS code that never arrived — already have a self-service answer that will resolve your problem faster than waiting for a reply.
  • If your account is frozen for a compliance review, we may be legally prevented from telling you why or when it will end. That is a constraint on us, not an oversight.

No one from Betabase will offer to "jump the queue" for a fee, and we will never contact you first through a DM.
