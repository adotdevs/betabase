How we verify your identity when you contact us

We verify you before discussing your account, because the alternative would let anyone claiming to be you take it over.

How verification usually works

  • Signed-in contact is the strongest. Opening a chat or ticket from inside your logged-in account authenticates you automatically, and requires the least extra checking.
  • Email from your registered address is the next best. We reply to that address only.
  • Additional checks may be requested for sensitive actions — changing your email or phone number, resetting 2FA, or unfreezing an account. These can include an in-app confirmation, a code to your registered email or phone, or a fresh liveness/document check.

What we will ask for

  • Confirmation of details already on file — for example your registered email, or the date and amount of a recent transaction.
  • A one-time code that we send to you, which you then enter in the app or on www.betabase.pro — never read aloud to an agent.
  • A document check, for high-risk changes.

What we will never ask for

  • Your password.
  • Your 2FA code, read out or typed into a chat.
  • Your seed phrase or private keys.
  • Remote-access or screen-sharing software to be installed.
  • Payment of a "verification fee", or a "test transaction" to prove ownership of a wallet.

Good to know

  • Account recovery is deliberately slow when you've lost both your password and your 2FA. That friction is what stops an attacker in the same position.
  • If a check feels excessive, that's usually a sign it is working.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. If anyone claiming to be from Betabase asks for any of these, they are a scammer. End the conversation and report it.
