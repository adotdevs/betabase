Daily and monthly transaction limits

Limits apply per rolling window — usually 24 hours, 30 days and sometimes 12 months — and are counted separately for deposits, withdrawals and, in some regions, trading.

How the windows work

  • A rolling 24-hour window counts everything in the last 24 hours from right now. It does not reset at midnight. A withdrawal made at 3pm yesterday frees up its allowance at 3pm today.
  • The 30-day window works the same way.
  • Because they roll, capacity returns gradually rather than all at once.

What counts toward what

  • Deposit limits count money coming in, per method. Cards often have a lower cap than bank transfers.
  • Withdrawal limits count EUR and crypto separately in most regions. Crypto limits are usually expressed as a EUR-equivalent value at the time of the withdrawal.
  • Trading limits, where they exist, count executed order value. Cancelled orders do not count.
  • Reserved funds on an open order do not consume a limit until the order actually fills.

If you hit a limit

  1. Check Profile → Limits to see exactly which limit is exhausted and when capacity returns.
  2. Wait for the window to roll, or
  3. Apply for a higher tier under Increase my limits.

Good to know

  • Limits may be tightened temporarily during a security review of your account.
  • Payment providers and your own bank apply their own separate caps, which Betabase cannot change.
