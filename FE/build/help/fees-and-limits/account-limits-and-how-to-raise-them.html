Account limits and how to raise them

Your limits are set by your verification tier. Completing more verification steps and providing source-of-funds evidence raises them.

Where to look

Open Profile → Limits. You will see each limit, how much of it you have used, and when the window resets.

Steps to raise a limit

  1. Go to Profile → Limits and select Increase my limits.
  2. Complete any outstanding verification: identity, proof of address, or additional questions.
  3. Answer the source-of-funds questionnaire — how you earned the money, your employment status, expected activity.
  4. Upload supporting evidence where requested. Typical documents: recent payslips, an employment contract, a bank statement showing the funds, a sale or inheritance document, or company accounts for business income.
  5. Submit. Reviews are usually completed within a few business days; complex cases take longer.
  6. You are notified by email, and the new limits appear on the Limits page.

Good to know

  • Limits are a regulatory requirement, not an arbitrary restriction. Betabase must know its customers and the origin of their funds.
  • Documents must be legible, in date, and in your own name. Screenshots with cropped edges are a frequent cause of rejection.
  • A higher limit is never guaranteed. Betabase can also lower a limit if risk indicators change.
  • Support cannot override a limit on request. The review process is the only route.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys as part of a limit review. Upload documents only inside your logged-in account.
