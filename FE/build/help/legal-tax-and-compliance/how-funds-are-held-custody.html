How your funds are held and safeguarded (custody explained)

Your cash and your crypto are held differently: cash is held in segregated accounts at partner banks, and crypto is held custodially, with the majority of assets kept in cold storage.

Cash

  • Client cash is held in segregated accounts, separate from Acme Technologies Ltd's own operating funds. It is not used to run the business.
  • Segregation means that, in an insolvency, client cash is intended to be identifiable as yours rather than forming part of the company's assets.
  • Whether a deposit-guarantee scheme applies depends on the partner bank and your jurisdiction. Check Legal → Safeguarding on www.betabase.pro for the current position.

Crypto

  • Crypto balances are custodial: Betabase holds the private keys on your behalf. You do not hold a seed phrase for your Betabase balance.
  • Client crypto is held separately from company assets and is recorded against your account in our books.
  • The large majority of client crypto is held in cold storage — offline, with multi-signature controls, hardware security modules and strict withdrawal approvals. A small operational float is kept in hot wallets to process withdrawals.
  • Crypto assets are generally not covered by deposit-guarantee or investor-compensation schemes. The Terms of Service set out exactly how assets are held.

Good to know

  • If you want to hold your own keys, withdraw to a self-custody wallet. You then bear full responsibility — a lost seed phrase means permanently lost funds.
  • We do not lend out or rehypothecate client crypto to fund the business.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys.
