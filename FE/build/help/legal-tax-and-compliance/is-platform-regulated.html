Is Betabase regulated?

Betabase is operated by Acme Technologies Ltd and is registered with, and supervised by, the relevant national financial authority for the activities it is authorised to carry out.

What this means

  • You contract with Acme Technologies Ltd. The full legal entity, registration number and registered address are shown in the Terms of Service and in the Imprint / Legal section of www.betabase.pro.
  • Regulation obliges us to meet requirements on anti-money-laundering controls, safeguarding of client assets, governance, record-keeping and complaint handling.
  • Our authorisation covers specific activities. Some products may be offered only in some countries, or by a different group entity, and this is stated at the point of use.

How to verify it yourself

  1. Open Legal → Regulatory information on www.betabase.pro and note the registration number.
  2. Look that number up directly on the public register maintained by the relevant national financial authority.
  3. Always start from the regulator's own website. Never trust a register link sent to you by a third party — cloned registers are a known scam.

Good to know — what regulation does not do

  • Being regulated does not make crypto a safe or low-risk investment. Prices remain volatile and your capital is at risk.
  • Crypto assets are generally not covered by deposit-guarantee or investor-compensation schemes in the way bank deposits are. Cash held for you may be treated differently from crypto — see How your funds are held and safeguarded.
  • Regulation does not protect you from losses caused by market movements, by your own transfer to a wrong address, or by a scam you authorised.
