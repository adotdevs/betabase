How to download your transaction history

Go to Transactions → Export, choose your date range and format, and the file is generated for download or emailed to you.

Steps

  1. On the dashboard, open Transactions.
  2. Select Export (or Download statement).
  3. Choose a date range. For a tax year, set it to the exact start and end dates your jurisdiction uses — these are not always 1 January to 31 December.
  4. Choose the transaction types to include: deposits, withdrawals, trades, swaps, fees, rewards and savings-plan executions. For a complete record, include everything.
  5. Choose the format: CSV for spreadsheets and tax software, PDF for a human-readable statement.
  6. Select Generate. Large exports are prepared in the background and emailed to you when ready.
  7. Open the file and check the row count against what you expect.

Good to know

  • The export is the authoritative record of your account. If something looks wrong in the interface, check the export first.
  • Exports are produced in your settlement currency, regardless of your display-currency setting.
  • CSV columns typically include timestamp, type, asset, quantity, price, fee and a unique transaction ID. Keep that ID — support needs it to trace anything.
  • Keep your own copies. Retain records for at least as long as your jurisdiction requires.

Your export contains sensitive financial data. Don't email it to anyone who contacts you claiming to be from Betabase or from a "tax recovery" service.
