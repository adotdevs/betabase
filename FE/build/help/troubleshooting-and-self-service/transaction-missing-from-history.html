My transaction is missing from my history

A "missing" transaction is nearly always hidden by an active filter, sitting outside the selected date range, or still pending — genuine data loss is extremely rare.

Steps

  1. Open Transactions and select Reset filters. Filters for asset, type and status persist between sessions and are the number one cause.
  2. Widen the date range to All time. Transactions are timestamped in a specific time zone, so an item made late at night can fall on the previous or next day.
  3. Include Pending in the status filter. Pending items are hidden from some default views.
  4. Pull down to refresh (app) or hard-refresh the page (web).
  5. Check the right account section — cash deposits, crypto transfers, trades and savings-plan executions can appear under separate tabs.
  6. If it's an incoming transfer, confirm it actually left the sending side: check your bank statement, or look up the transaction hash on a block explorer.
  7. Still nothing? Export your history (see How to download your transaction history) and search the file. The export is the authoritative record.

Good to know

  • Incoming crypto only appears once the network has produced the required number of confirmations. Until then it exists on-chain but not in your account.
  • Bank transfers sent without the correct reference code may not be matched to your account automatically and will need manual allocation.
  • Internal transfers between your own sections of the account may display as two linked entries rather than one.
