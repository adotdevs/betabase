I didn't receive the confirmation email

Confirmation emails are sent within seconds — if you don't see one, it is nearly always sitting in spam, caught by a filter, or heading to a mistyped address.

Steps

  1. Check Spam / Junk, and on Gmail also check the Promotions and Updates tabs.
  2. Search your mailbox for "Betabase" rather than scrolling — filters can move mail into folders you never open.
  3. Confirm you typed the address correctly at sign-up. A single wrong character means the email went somewhere else entirely.
  4. Add our sending domain to your safe senders / allow list in your email settings.
  5. Return to www.betabase.pro and select Resend confirmation email.
  6. If nothing arrives after 15 minutes, try registering with a different provider — some corporate and ISP mail servers silently reject automated mail.

Good to know

  • Confirmation links are single-use and expire after a short window. If you click an old link it will fail; request a fresh one.
  • Corporate mail servers frequently strip or quarantine external links. A personal address is more reliable.
  • If you cannot access the mailbox at all, you cannot self-serve an email change — support must verify your identity first.

Only ever click links in emails that arrive after you asked for them. An unexpected "confirm your account" email may be a phishing attempt. Type www.betabase.pro into your browser yourself instead.
