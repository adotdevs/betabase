How to report a suspicious email or website

Forward the message — with its full headers where possible — to support@www.betabase.pro, then delete it. Never click the links or open the attachments.

How to spot a fake

  • Urgency and threat. "Your account will be closed in 24 hours." Real notices don't create panic.
  • A near-miss domain. Hover over links and read the address carefully. Look for extra words, hyphens, swapped characters or unusual endings that only resemble www.betabase.pro.
  • Unexpected attachments, or a login page that appears the moment you click.
  • Requests for secrets. Any request for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys.
  • Sponsored search results. Fake sites buy ads above the real one. Type www.betabase.pro yourself or use a bookmark.

Steps

  1. Do not click, reply or download anything.
  2. Take a screenshot showing the sender address and the link target.
  3. Forward the original email to support@www.betabase.pro as an attachment where your mail client allows it — this preserves the headers we need.
  4. For a fake website, send us the full URL as plain text.
  5. Delete the message and block the sender.
  6. Report it to your national anti-fraud or cybercrime reporting service as well.

If you already clicked

  1. Do not enter anything. If you already did, change your password immediately and reset 2FA.
  2. Freeze your account and follow I think my account has been compromised — do this now.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. We will never ask you to install remote-access or screen-sharing software.
