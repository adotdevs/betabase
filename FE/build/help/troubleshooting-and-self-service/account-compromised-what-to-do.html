I think my account has been compromised — do this now

If you suspect unauthorised access, act immediately and in this order — speed matters far more than certainty. It is fine to be wrong.

Steps

  1. Freeze the account. Go to Settings → Security → Freeze my account (or use the self-service freeze link on the help-centre homepage). This blocks trading and withdrawals instantly.
  2. Change your password to a new, unique one you have never used anywhere else. Do this from a device you trust.
  3. Reset your 2FA. Remove the existing method and set up a new one. If an attacker has enrolled their own device, this cuts them off.
  4. Revoke all devices and sessions under Settings → Security → Devices. Sign out everywhere.
  5. Check your email account. Compromise usually starts there. Change that password too, enable 2FA on it, and delete any forwarding rules or filters you didn't create — attackers add them to hide alerts.
  6. Review withdrawal addresses in your address book and delete any you didn't add.
  7. Contact support immediately with the date and time you first noticed the activity, and any transaction IDs.
  8. Run a malware scan on any device you used to sign in.

Good to know

  • On-chain withdrawals are irreversible. Freezing early is the only thing that reliably stops loss.
  • Consider reporting the theft to your local police or cybercrime unit — you may need a report reference for any insurance or bank claim.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. No "recovery service" can reverse a blockchain transaction. Anyone promising to recover stolen crypto for an upfront fee is running a second scam on you.
