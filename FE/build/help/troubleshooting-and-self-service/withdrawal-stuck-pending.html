A withdrawal is stuck as pending

A pending withdrawal is normally waiting on one of three things: your own email/2FA confirmation, a routine security hold, or blockchain/banking settlement.

Steps

  1. Check your email. Many withdrawals require you to confirm the request from a link. Until you click it, nothing is sent. Check spam.
  2. Open Transactions and select the withdrawal to see its exact status.
  3. If the status shows a security hold, this is standard — new withdrawal addresses, new devices and unusually large amounts trigger a cooling-off period, typically up to 24–48 hours. It exists to protect you if your account is ever compromised.
  4. If the status shows sent or broadcast and gives a transaction hash, Betabase's part is finished. Paste the hash into a block explorer to see where it is on the network. A low network fee during congestion means a slow confirmation.
  5. For a cash withdrawal shown as sent, allow 1–2 business days for your bank to post it. Banks do not settle at weekends.
  6. If it is outside those windows, contact support with the transaction ID.

Good to know

  • Withdrawals can be paused if your verification has lapsed, your limits are exhausted, or a compliance check is open. You'll normally see a prompt telling you what's needed.
  • Once an on-chain transaction is broadcast it cannot be cancelled, reversed or recalled — not by you, not by Betabase.
  • You cannot withdraw funds that are still unsettled from a recent deposit.

There is no way to "speed up" a withdrawal by paying a fee to a person. Anyone offering that is a scammer.
