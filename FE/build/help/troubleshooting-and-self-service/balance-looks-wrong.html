My balance looks wrong

Nine times out of ten a "wrong" balance is a display-currency setting, an unsettled pending item, or normal price movement — not missing money.

Steps

  1. Check your display currency in Profile → Settings → Preferences. Display currency is cosmetic: it changes how values are shown, using an indicative FX rate, and does not convert your holdings.
  2. Look for Pending items in Transactions. Pending deposits are not yet spendable; pending withdrawals are already deducted from your available balance.
  3. Distinguish available balance from total balance. Funds locked in an open limit order, a savings plan or a staking position are part of your total but not available to spend.
  4. Remember that crypto prices move constantly. A portfolio value that changed overnight is normal market movement, not an error.
  5. Check whether fees were applied — trading fees, network fees and spread are shown on each transaction's detail page.
  6. Export your transaction history and reconcile line by line. Every credit and debit is itemised.

Good to know

  • Percentage figures on the dashboard are shown against the time range you selected, not against your purchase price. Personal profit/loss is on the individual asset page.
  • Rounding in a non-settlement display currency can make small balances look slightly off. Your settlement currency figure is the accurate one.
  • Crypto is volatile and your capital is at risk. A falling balance is not a malfunction.

If you find transactions you did not make, treat it as a compromise immediately — see I think my account has been compromised — do this now.
