How long does verification take?

Most automated identity checks complete within a few minutes. Checks that go to manual review typically finish within 1–3 business days.

Typical timings

  • Automated ID check: minutes. You usually have the result before you close the app.
  • Manual review: 1–3 business days. Triggered by a borderline photo, an unusual document, or a name mismatch.
  • Proof of address: 1–3 business days.
  • Source of funds / enhanced checks: 5–10 business days, because a compliance specialist reads every document.

Why a check takes longer

  • The document photo was borderline — readable, but not cleanly.
  • Your name, date of birth or address does not exactly match across documents.
  • Your document type is uncommon in our system and needs a human to confirm.
  • We are experiencing high volume, typically during a market rally.

Good to know

  • Business days exclude weekends and public holidays. A Friday-evening submission is realistically a Monday or Tuesday answer.
  • Submitting the same check repeatedly does not speed it up — it re-queues you at the back and can flag the account. Submit once and wait.
  • You are emailed the moment the status changes. There is no need to check hourly.
  • If you have heard nothing after 5 business days, contact support at support@www.betabase.pro with your account email and the date you submitted. Support is available 24/7.
