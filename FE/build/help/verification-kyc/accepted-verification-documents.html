Which documents are accepted

Betabase accepts a valid, unexpired, government-issued photo ID: normally a passport, a national ID card, or — in some countries — a driving licence or residence permit.

Accepted

  • Passport — the photo page. Accepted in every country we serve and the most reliable choice.
  • National ID card — front and back. Must be a card, not a paper certificate.
  • Driving licence — front and back, and only where your country's licence is on our accepted list.
  • Residence permit — accepted in selected countries where it carries a photo and machine-readable zone.

Not accepted

  • Expired documents, even by one day.
  • Photocopies, scans of photocopies, or a photo of a document on a screen.
  • Documents with the photo, name, date of birth or document number obscured, cropped or covered by a finger.
  • Birth certificates, student cards, library cards, work badges, tax cards, health-insurance cards.
  • Provisional or temporary paper licences.
  • Documents in a name that does not exactly match the name on your Betabase account.

Good to know

  • The document must be the physical original, held in your hand, in front of the camera.
  • All four corners must be visible, in frame, with no glare across the text.
  • If your legal name has changed, update it in Profile → Personal details before you verify, and be ready to supply the change-of-name document.
  • Some countries require a second document. If so, the flow tells you before you start.
