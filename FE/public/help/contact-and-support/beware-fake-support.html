Beware of fake support: Betabase will never DM you first

Betabase will never contact you first by direct message. Not on Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord, X, Instagram, Reddit or by phone. Anyone who does is a scammer, without exception.

How the scam works

Scammers watch for anyone posting publicly about a crypto problem — a stuck withdrawal, a login issue, a complaint. Within minutes, an account with our logo and a near-identical name messages you "to help". Others buy search adverts so their cloned site sits above the real one. They are patient, polite, professional and extremely convincing. Sounding legitimate is the whole job.

Red flags — every one of these means stop

  • Anyone who messaged you first.
  • A request for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys.
  • A request to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer or any other remote-access or screen-sharing tool.
  • A link to a "wallet validation", "sync", "restore" or "migration" page.
  • A demand for a fee, deposit or "test transaction" to release your funds, verify your wallet, or speed up a withdrawal.
  • Pressure and urgency: "act now or your funds will be lost."
  • An offer to recover funds you already lost. This is a second scam targeting victims of the first.

What to do

  1. Do not reply. Screenshot the profile and the messages.
  2. Block and report the account to the platform it's on.
  3. Forward the details to support@www.betabase.pro.
  4. If you shared anything at all, follow I think my account has been compromised — do this now immediately.
  5. Only ever contact us from inside the Betabase app or by typing www.betabase.pro yourself.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. No one can reverse a confirmed blockchain transaction — anyone who says they can is lying to you.
