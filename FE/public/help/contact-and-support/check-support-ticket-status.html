How to check the status of your support ticket

Every request gets a reference number by email. Use it to track the ticket under Help → My requests when signed in.

Steps

  1. Sign in at www.betabase.pro or open the Betabase app.
  2. Go to Help → My requests (or Support → My tickets).
  3. Find your ticket by its reference number — it's in the confirmation email you received when you submitted it.
  4. Read the status:

- Open — received and queued. - In progress — an agent is actively working on it. - Awaiting your reply — we need something from you. The ticket will not move until you respond, and may auto-close if you don't. - Solved / Closed — we consider it resolved.

  1. To add information, reply on the existing thread. Don't open a new ticket.

Good to know

  • Awaiting your reply is the most common reason a ticket appears to stall. Check whether the ball is in your court before you chase.
  • Replies also arrive by email. Check your spam folder — support replies are frequently filtered.
  • A closed ticket can usually be reopened by replying to it, as long as it's the same issue. A different issue needs a new ticket.
  • Chasing does not accelerate a ticket, but adding a genuinely new detail (a transaction ID you'd omitted, for instance) often does.

Support replies come only through the in-app ticket thread or from our official domain. A "reply" arriving via DM, WhatsApp or Telegram is not from us.
