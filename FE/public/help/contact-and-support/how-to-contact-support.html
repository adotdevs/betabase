How to contact Betabase support

Contact us from inside your logged-in account — that is the only way we can be sure we're talking to the real account holder, and it's the fastest route to a resolution.

Steps

  1. Sign in at www.betabase.pro or open the Betabase app.
  2. Select the Help or Support icon.
  3. Search the help centre first. Most issues — missing codes, pending withdrawals, wrong-looking balances — have a self-service answer that is faster than waiting for a reply.
  4. If that doesn't resolve it, select Contact us and choose your topic. The topic determines which team receives it.
  5. Choose your channel:

- Live chat — best for account-specific, urgent issues. Availability follows 24/7. - Support ticket / contact form — best for anything needing documents, screenshots or investigation. - Email support@www.betabase.pro — use the address registered to your account, or we can't verify you.

  1. Fill in the form completely (see What information to include in your support request) and submit.

Good to know

  • Contacting us from a signed-in session means you're already partially verified, which cuts the identity checks and speeds everything up.
  • If you're locked out entirely, email support@www.betabase.pro from your registered address and expect additional identity verification.
  • We do not provide support over social media DMs, Telegram, WhatsApp or Discord, and we do not give investment advice.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Always start from www.betabase.pro or the Betabase app — never from a link or phone number found through a search advert or a social media post.
