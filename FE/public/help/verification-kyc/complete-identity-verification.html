How to complete identity verification step by step

Start verification from your dashboard, scan your ID with your phone camera, complete a short liveness selfie, and confirm your personal details — it usually takes under ten minutes.

Before you start

  • Your physical ID document, unexpired, in your hand.
  • A phone with a working camera. Doing this on a phone is far more reliable than a laptop webcam.
  • Good, even lighting — daylight is ideal. Avoid backlight from a window behind you.
  • Your name on Betabase must match your ID exactly.

Steps

  1. Sign in and select Verify now on the dashboard, or go to Profile → Verification.
  2. Choose your country of issue and your document type.
  3. Choose to continue on your phone. Scan the QR code shown on screen, or open the Betabase app and tap Verification.
  4. Allow camera access when prompted.
  5. Photograph the front of the document. Hold steady until the frame turns green, then confirm.
  6. Photograph the back, if your document type requires it.
  7. Complete the liveness check: look at the camera and follow the on-screen prompts — turn your head, blink, or read out a number.
  8. Review the details we have read from your document — name, date of birth, document number, expiry. Correct anything the scan misread.
  9. Select Submit. You return to Betabase and the status changes to In review.

Good to know

  • Do not close the app or browser while the check uploads.
  • If the automated check cannot decide, a human reviews it — this adds time but is not a rejection.
  • You get an email as soon as the result is in. Check the status any time under Profile → Verification.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Only ever verify inside the Betabase app or on www.betabase.pro while logged in.
