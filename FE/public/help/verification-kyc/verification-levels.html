Verification levels and what each one unlocks

Betabase uses tiered verification: each level you complete unlocks more of the product and raises your deposit and withdrawal limits.

The tiers

  • Registered. Email confirmed only. You can browse markets, watch prices and set up alerts. You cannot deposit, trade or withdraw.
  • Verified. Identity check passed. This unlocks deposits, buying, selling, swapping and withdrawals up to your standard limits. This is the level most users need.
  • Verified +. Proof of address added, and in some countries a tax identification number. This raises your limits and unlocks higher-value features.
  • Enhanced. For high-volume accounts. Requires source of funds and sometimes source of wealth evidence — for example payslips, a sale contract, a dividend statement or an accountant's letter. Unlocks the highest limits.

Steps to upgrade

  1. Go to Profile → Verification. Your current level is shown at the top.
  2. Select Upgrade next to the next level.
  3. Complete the additional checks listed for that tier.
  4. Limits update automatically once the review passes.

Good to know

  • Your exact limits are always shown under Profile → Limits, and they vary by country, payment method and account age.
  • Source-of-funds checks are a legal requirement, not a judgement about you. Documents are reviewed by a compliance specialist, which takes longer than an automated ID scan.
  • We may ask you to re-confirm details periodically even if nothing has changed. This is routine.
  • Downgrades can happen if a document expires — re-upload it to restore your limits.
