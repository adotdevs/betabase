Verification for a business/company account

Business accounts go through a separate process called KYB (Know Your Business), which verifies the company itself, its directors, and everyone who ultimately owns or controls it.

Before you start

Gather digital copies of:

  • Certificate of incorporation and a current extract from the commercial or companies register (usually dated within 3 months).
  • Articles of association or equivalent constitutional document.
  • Ownership structure chart, showing the chain of ownership up to the ultimate beneficial owners (UBOs) — normally anyone holding more than 25%.
  • ID and proof of address for every director and every UBO. Each individual completes the same identity check as a personal customer.
  • Proof of a corporate bank account in the company's name.
  • Evidence of business activity — a website, licence, contracts or invoices — plus an explanation of the expected source of funds and trading volume.

Steps

  1. Apply through the Business accounts page on www.betabase.pro. Do not attempt to use a personal account for company activity.
  2. Complete the entity questionnaire: legal name, registration number, registered address, industry, expected volumes.
  3. Upload the corporate documents above.
  4. Invite each director and UBO. Each receives a link and completes their own identity check.
  5. A compliance specialist reviews the file and may come back with follow-up questions.
  6. Once approved, the account is activated and limits are set based on your declared activity.

Good to know

  • KYB typically takes 2–4 weeks, sometimes longer for complex or multi-jurisdiction structures. Answering follow-up questions promptly is the single biggest factor in how fast it closes.
  • Only a corporate bank account in the company's name may fund the account. Payments from a director's personal account are returned.
  • Businesses in some sectors and jurisdictions cannot be onboarded at all, regardless of documents.
  • Company details must be kept current — tell us when directors or ownership change.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Corporate documents are uploaded only inside the logged-in business portal.
