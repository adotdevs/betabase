How to submit proof of address

Proof of address is a document, dated within the last three months, that shows your full name and your current residential address — most often a utility bill or a bank statement.

Accepted documents

  • Utility bill: electricity, gas, water, landline or broadband.
  • Bank or credit-card statement from a regulated bank.
  • Council tax, local authority or government tax letter.
  • Official government correspondence (social security, electoral roll, residence registration).
  • Tenancy agreement or mortgage statement, in some countries.

Not accepted

  • Mobile phone bills (in most countries).
  • Insurance quotes, delivery notes, invoices from shops, handwritten letters.
  • Documents older than three months, or with no visible issue date.
  • Screenshots, cropped images, or documents with the address blacked out.
  • A document in someone else's name — including a partner or parent.

Steps

  1. Go to Profile → Verification → Proof of address.
  2. Select the document type.
  3. Upload a full-page PDF or photo. Do not crop. The name, address, issuer logo and date must all be visible on the same page.
  4. If you are using an online statement, download the original PDF from your bank or provider — do not screenshot the browser.
  5. Select Submit and wait for the status to change.

Good to know

  • The address on the document must match the address on your Betabase profile exactly. Update your profile first if you have moved.
  • Not everyone is asked for proof of address. It is triggered by your country, your limit tier, or a mismatch on your ID.
  • Reviews typically complete within 1–3 business days.
