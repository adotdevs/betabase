My verification was rejected — what now?

A rejection almost always means a document or photo problem, not a judgement about you — read the reason in the rejection email, fix that one thing, and resubmit.

Common reasons and fixes

  • Photo unreadable (glare, blur, cropped). Retake it on a dark matte surface in indirect daylight, with all four corners in frame.
  • Document expired. Renew it. There is no workaround — an expired document cannot be accepted.
  • Name mismatch. The name on your account must exactly match the name on your ID, including middle names and accents. Fix it in Profile → Personal details, then resubmit.
  • Address mismatch. Update your address in your profile so it matches your proof of address.
  • Liveness check failed. Redo it in even light, no hat, no glasses, plain background, and follow every on-screen prompt.
  • Document type not accepted in your country. Use a passport instead — it is accepted everywhere we operate.

Steps

  1. Open the rejection email or go to Profile → Verification. The specific reason is shown there.
  2. Fix that reason. Do not simply resubmit the same images.
  3. Select Try again and complete the flow.
  4. If you have been rejected twice for the same reason and cannot see why, contact support@www.betabase.pro with your account email and the date of the attempt.

Good to know

  • There is no penalty for a failed attempt, but repeated identical resubmissions can lock the flow temporarily.
  • Rejections for compliance reasons — for example a sanctions match or a restricted country — cannot be appealed by resubmitting documents. Support will explain the next step.

Nobody can "fast-track", "unlock" or "guarantee" verification for a fee. Anyone offering that is a scammer. Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys.
