Why do I need to verify my identity? (KYC/AML explained)

Betabase is a regulated financial service, and the law requires us to confirm the identity of every customer before they can deposit, trade or withdraw.

What KYC and AML actually mean

  • KYC (Know Your Customer) is the process of proving you are a real person, that you are who you say you are, and that you live where you say you live.
  • AML (Anti-Money Laundering) is the wider set of rules that oblige regulated firms to detect and prevent funds moving through them illegally.

Every licensed exchange, broker and bank in a regulated market runs the same checks. A platform that lets you deposit and withdraw large sums with no identity check is either unlicensed or breaking the law — and your funds are far less safe there.

What verification protects

  • You. A verified account is much harder for a fraudster to take over or impersonate.
  • Your money. Verification is what lets us return funds to the right person if something goes wrong.
  • The platform. Without it, Betabase would lose its licence and you would lose access to your balances.

Good to know

  • You can create an account and browse markets before verifying. You cannot deposit, buy, sell or withdraw until it is complete.
  • Your documents are encrypted, stored securely and handled under our Privacy Policy. They are never sold or used for marketing.
  • We may re-run checks later — for example if you move country, change your name, or increase your transaction volume. This is normal and not an accusation.
  • Refusing to complete verification means the account stays restricted and can eventually be closed with funds returned to a verified bank account in your name.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Verification asks only for identity documents, uploaded inside your logged-in account — never by email, chat or WhatsApp.
