I'm stuck in a verification loop

A verification loop — where the check restarts instead of completing — is almost always caused by an unreadable document image or a blocked camera permission, not by a rejection.

Before you start

  • Have your physical, unexpired ID document. Photocopies, screenshots and scans are rejected automatically.
  • Move to a well-lit room. Avoid direct light on the document.

Steps

  1. Use the Betabase app rather than a laptop webcam. Phone cameras pass far more often.
  2. Grant camera permission when prompted. If you previously denied it, re-enable it in your device settings — a denied camera silently restarts the flow.
  3. Lay the document flat on a plain, dark, non-reflective surface. Remove any card holder or plastic sleeve.
  4. Fill the frame: all four corners visible, no fingers over the edges, no glare on the hologram.
  5. For the selfie/liveness step, remove glasses, hats and masks. Face a window, not a lamp.
  6. Complete the flow in one sitting. Switching apps or letting the screen lock mid-check can reset it.
  7. If it still loops after three genuine attempts, stop. Repeated attempts do not help and can trigger a temporary block. Contact support with the time of your last attempt.

Good to know

  • Check that the name and date of birth in your profile match your ID character for character. A mismatch causes a silent restart.
  • VPNs and privacy browsers frequently break the verification frame. Disable them for this step.
  • Verification is normally reviewed quickly, but manual review can take longer during busy periods.
