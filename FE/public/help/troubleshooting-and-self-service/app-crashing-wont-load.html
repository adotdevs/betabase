The app keeps crashing or won't load

Most crashes are fixed by updating the Betabase app to the latest version and restarting your device — an outdated build is by far the most common cause.

Steps

  1. Open the App Store or Google Play and check whether an update to the Betabase app is pending. Install it.
  2. Fully close the app (swipe it away from the app switcher) and reopen it. Don't just background it.
  3. Restart your phone. This clears stuck memory and network state.
  4. Check your connection. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data — a captive portal or corporate Wi-Fi can silently block the app's traffic.
  5. Clear the app cache. Android: Settings → Apps → the Betabase app → Storage → Clear cache (not Clear data). iOS: offload and reinstall.
  6. Confirm your operating system meets the minimum version listed on the store page. Very old OS versions are unsupported.
  7. If it still crashes, uninstall and reinstall. Your account and balances are held server-side and are never stored only on the device.
  8. As a workaround, sign in on the web at www.betabase.pro — full functionality is available there.

Good to know

  • Reinstalling signs you out. Have your 2FA method to hand before you delete the app, or you may lock yourself out.
  • If the app opens but shows a blank portfolio, that's usually a connectivity issue, not lost funds. Your balances are safe on the server.
  • Jailbroken or rooted devices are not supported and may be blocked for security reasons.
