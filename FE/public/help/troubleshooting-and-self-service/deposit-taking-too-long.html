A deposit is taking longer than expected

Card and instant payments usually credit within minutes; standard bank transfers typically take 1–2 business days; crypto arrives after the network reaches the required confirmations.

Before you start

Check what actually applies to your deposit:

  • Bank transfer: 1–2 business days. Nothing moves at weekends or on bank holidays. A payment sent Friday evening may not settle until Tuesday.
  • Card / instant payment: minutes, occasionally up to an hour.
  • Crypto: minutes to over an hour, depending on the network, its congestion, and the fee the sender paid.

Steps

  1. Confirm the money actually left the sender: check your bank statement or, for crypto, look up the transaction hash on a block explorer.
  2. For bank transfers, check you included the reference code exactly as shown. A missing, edited or truncated reference is the single most common cause of a delayed deposit — it must be matched manually.
  3. Confirm the sending account is in your own name. Third-party payments are rejected and returned, which takes days and may cost a fee.
  4. For crypto, confirm you used the correct network and the exact deposit address from your logged-in account.
  5. Wait out the normal window above before contacting support.
  6. If it's outside the window, contact support with the amount, date, sending account and the reference or transaction hash.

Good to know

  • Larger or first-time deposits may be held briefly for routine compliance checks. This is normal and not a sign of a problem.
  • Network fees are set by the blockchain, not by Betabase. A low fee paid by the sender means a slow confirmation.

Only ever send money to the account details or deposit address shown inside your logged-in account. Details sent by email, chat or phone are a scam.
