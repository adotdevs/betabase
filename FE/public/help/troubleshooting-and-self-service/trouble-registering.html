I'm having trouble registering

Registration usually fails for one of four reasons: the email is already registered, your country isn't supported, you don't meet the age requirement, or your browser is blocking part of the flow.

Steps

  1. Already registered? Go to Sign in → Forgot password with the same email. If you get a reset link, the account exists — sign in rather than re-register.
  2. Check your country. Open Legal → Supported countries on www.betabase.pro. If your country of residence isn't listed, registration will not complete.
  3. Check your date of birth. You must be 18 or over. A mistyped year is a common cause of a hard block.
  4. Try a clean browser session. Disable ad blockers and privacy extensions, clear cookies for www.betabase.pro, or open a private window. These commonly break captchas and embedded verification frames.
  5. Try the other channel. If the web form fails, register in the Betabase app — or vice versa.
  6. If you see a specific error code, note it exactly and include it in your support request.

Good to know

  • One person may hold one account. Duplicate registrations are detected and closed.
  • A VPN can cause registration to fail or to be flagged, because it makes your location look inconsistent with your address. Turn it off.
  • Your name must match your ID exactly — you can't fix a mismatched name later without a document check.

Never register through a link sent to you by a stranger, an "advisor" or a social media account. Type www.betabase.pro in yourself.
