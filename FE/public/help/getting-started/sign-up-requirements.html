What you need to sign up (age, ID, country)

To open a Betabase account you must be at least 18 years old, live in a supported country, and hold a valid government-issued photo ID.

Before you start

Have these ready:

  • Age: 18 or over. There are no junior or custodial accounts.
  • Residency: a permanent address in a country Betabase serves. Your address must match your ID or your proof of address.
  • Identity document: a valid, unexpired passport, national ID card or (in some countries) a driving licence. It must be the physical document — photocopies and scans of scans are rejected.
  • Proof of address: sometimes requested — a utility bill, bank statement or council/tax letter dated within the last 3 months.
  • Tax details: depending on your country we may ask for a tax identification number.

Good to know

  • Your name on Betabase must exactly match your ID. Nicknames and shortened names cause verification to fail.
  • You must fund the account from a bank account in your own name. Third-party payments are returned.
  • Sanctioned individuals and residents of restricted jurisdictions cannot register, and accounts are screened continuously.
  • Politically exposed persons (PEPs) can usually register but may face extra checks.

Providing false information is grounds for immediate account closure and may be reported to authorities.
