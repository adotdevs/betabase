Countries Betabase is available in

Betabase is available in a defined list of countries, published on the Supported countries page at www.betabase.pro — and the list changes as licensing and regulation evolve.

Steps

  1. Go to www.betabase.pro and open Legal → Supported countries.
  2. Find your country of residence in the list.
  3. Check the notes column — some countries have access to only part of the product range.
  4. If your country is not listed, you cannot open an account. Do not attempt to register with a different country; that violates the Terms of Service.

Good to know

  • Availability is based on your country of residence, not your nationality or where you happen to be travelling.
  • Individual features (staking, savings plans, certain assets, card products) may be unavailable in a supported country even when the account itself works.
  • If you move to a new country, update your address in Profile → Personal details. We may ask for a new proof of address and re-run checks.
  • If you move to a country we do not serve, your account moves to withdrawal-only: you can sell and withdraw your balances, but not buy or deposit.
  • Using a VPN to disguise your location breaches the Terms of Service and can result in the account being frozen.
