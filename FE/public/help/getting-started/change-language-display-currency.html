How to change your language and display currency

Change both in Profile → Settings → Preferences: pick your interface language and the currency your portfolio value is shown in.

Steps

  1. Select your profile icon, then Settings.
  2. Open Preferences (called App settings in the Betabase app).
  3. Under Language, choose from the supported languages. The interface updates immediately.
  4. Under Display currency, choose the currency you want values shown in.
  5. Changes save automatically and apply to both web and app.

Good to know

  • Display currency is cosmetic. It changes how values are shown. It does not convert your balances and it does not change the currency you actually hold or trade in.
  • Your account's settlement currency stays EUR. Deposits, withdrawals and fees are still processed in EUR.
  • Values shown in a non-settlement display currency use an indicative FX rate and are rounded.
  • Statements and tax exports are generated in EUR regardless of your display setting.
  • Emails and legal documents may remain in English where a translation is not available.
