How to make your first deposit

Add money by opening Deposit, choosing a payment method, entering an amount of at least €10, and following the instructions for that method.

Before you start

  • Your identity verification must be complete.
  • The bank account or card must be in your own name. Third-party payments are rejected and returned, which takes time and may cost a fee.

Steps

  1. On the dashboard, select Deposit.
  2. Choose your currency — normally EUR.
  3. Choose a payment method: bank transfer, instant bank payment, or debit/credit card. Available methods depend on your country.
  4. Enter the amount. The minimum is €10.
  5. For a bank transfer, copy the account details and the reference code exactly, then make the payment from your banking app.
  6. For a card or instant payment, follow the on-screen flow and approve the payment with your bank.
  7. Return to Betabase. The deposit appears under Transactions with a status of Pending until it settles.

Good to know

  • Card and instant payments usually credit within minutes. Standard bank transfers typically take 1–2 business days and do not move at weekends or on bank holidays.
  • The reference code links your payment to your account. A missing or altered reference is the single most common cause of a delayed deposit.
  • Fees depend on the method — check Fees & limits before you choose.

Only ever send money to the account details shown inside your logged-in Betabase account. Details sent to you by email, chat or phone are a scam.
