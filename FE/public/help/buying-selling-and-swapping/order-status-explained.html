Order status: pending, filled, partially filled, cancelled

Every order carries a status that tells you exactly where it is. You'll find it under Transactions → Orders.

The statuses

  • Pending / Open. Received and live in the market. A market order is pending for seconds; a limit or stop order can be open indefinitely. Funds are reserved.
  • Pending trigger. A stop order that is waiting for its trigger price. It is not in the market yet.
  • Partially filled. Some of the quantity has executed; the rest is still open. You already own the filled part, and the remaining amount stays reserved. Cancelling now keeps the filled part and releases the rest.
  • Filled. Fully executed. The asset is in your portfolio and the order is final.
  • Cancelled. Cancelled by you, or automatically at expiry. Reserved funds are released, normally within moments.
  • Rejected. Never entered the market — usually insufficient balance, below the minimum, a limit breach, or a suspended market. The reason is shown on the order detail.
  • Expired. Reached the end of its time-in-force window without filling.

Good to know

  • A filled order cannot be cancelled, reversed or amended. Finality is immediate.
  • Reserved funds do not appear as available balance. If your available balance looks low, check for open orders.
  • A partially filled limit order can fill in many small pieces over time. Each piece shows on the order detail; fees are charged on what actually executes.
