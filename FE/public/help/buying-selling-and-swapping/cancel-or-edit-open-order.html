How to cancel or edit an open order

You can cancel any order that has not yet filled from Transactions → Open orders. To change a price or amount, you cancel the order and place a new one.

Steps

  1. Go to Transactions → Open orders (or Orders in the Betabase app).
  2. Find the order. Check its status is Open, Pending or Pending trigger.
  3. Select the order to open its detail view.
  4. Select Cancel order and confirm.
  5. The status changes to Cancelled and any reserved cash or crypto returns to your available balance.

Good to know

  • Most platforms do not support in-place editing of a live order. Cancel and replace is the standard method — and it is what the Edit button does behind the scenes where it exists.
  • A market order cannot be cancelled. It executes in a fraction of a second; by the time you can tap anything, it has filled.
  • Cancelling a partially filled order cancels only the unfilled remainder. The filled part is yours and stays.
  • There is no fee to cancel or place an order. Fees are charged only on execution.
  • If a cancel request arrives at the same moment the order fills, the fill wins. You will see Filled, not Cancelled, and that is final.

Cancelling and replacing means losing your place in the queue. In a fast market the new order may fill at a very different price.
