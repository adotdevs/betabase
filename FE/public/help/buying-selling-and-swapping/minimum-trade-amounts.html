Minimum trade amounts

Every asset has a minimum order size — typically expressed as a small EUR value such as €1 — below which an order is rejected.

Where to check

  1. Open the asset and start a Buy or Sell.
  2. The minimum is shown under the amount field, and again in the error message if you go below it.
  3. Full per-asset minimums are also listed on the Fees & limits page at www.betabase.pro.

Why minimums exist

  • Very small orders cost more to process and settle than they are worth, and rounding at the asset's price precision can make them impossible to execute accurately.
  • Some assets have high price precision or high network fees, so their minimum is higher than others.

Dealing with dust

  • Dust is a leftover balance too small to sell — often the residue of a partial fill, a reward, or a price move after a "sell all".
  • Use Sell all on the asset page rather than typing a quantity manually. It handles the final decimals for you.
  • If a balance is genuinely below the minimum, it simply stays in your portfolio. It is not lost, and it becomes sellable again if the asset's price rises enough.

Good to know

  • Minimums can change without notice as prices and network costs move.
  • Savings plans have their own minimum instalment, which can differ from the one-off trade minimum.
