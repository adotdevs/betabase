How to sell cryptocurrency

Selling converts crypto back into EUR: open the asset, select Sell, enter how much you want to sell, review the quote and confirm. The proceeds land in your cash balance.

Before you start

  • The crypto must be settled in your Betabase portfolio. Incoming on-chain deposits need their confirmations first.
  • Assets locked in staking or an open order are not sellable until they are unlocked or the order is cancelled.

Steps

  1. Open Portfolio and select the asset you hold.
  2. Select Sell.
  3. Enter the amount — either a quantity of the asset or a EUR value. Sell all sells the full available balance.
  4. Choose Market or Limit.
  5. Review the summary: EUR you receive, price, spread and fee.
  6. Select Confirm.

Good to know

  • Selling puts EUR into your cash balance on Betabase. It does not send money to your bank — that is a separate withdrawal.
  • Proceeds from a sale are normally available to trade again immediately, and to withdraw once any holding period has passed.
  • Selling may be a taxable event in your country. Betabase does not give tax advice; export your transaction history and speak to a professional.

Selling is irreversible once the order fills. Betabase cannot undo a filled order because the price moved afterwards.
