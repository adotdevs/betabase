Understanding your portfolio, average buy price and total return

Your average buy price is the weighted average of everything you paid for an asset, and your total return compares that against its current value.

How the numbers work

  • Average buy price = total EUR spent on that asset (including fees, where shown) ÷ total quantity held. Every new buy pulls the average towards the price you just paid.
  • Unrealised return = current value − (average buy price × quantity held). It changes every second with the market and only becomes real when you sell.
  • Realised return = the profit or loss on units you have already sold.
  • Total return combines the two, and where relevant includes rewards received.
  • Selling part of a holding does not change your average buy price — it reduces the quantity, not the cost per unit.

Good to know

  • Percentage figures on the dashboard show the market's move over a selected time range. The figure on the asset page shows your return. They will differ, and that is expected.
  • Crypto received from staking rewards or promotions may enter at a cost basis of zero, which lowers your average.
  • Values shown in a display currency other than EUR use an indicative FX rate and are rounded.
  • These figures are indicative and are not a tax calculation. For tax, export your full transaction history.

Past returns say nothing about future returns. Your capital is at risk.
