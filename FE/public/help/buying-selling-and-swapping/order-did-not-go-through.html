My order didn't go through — troubleshooting

Most failed orders come down to one of six causes. Work through them in order.

1. Insufficient available balance

Open orders reserve funds. Your total balance can look healthy while your available balance is zero. Go to Transactions → Open orders and cancel anything you no longer want. Remember the fee must fit inside the balance too.

2. Unsettled funds

A deposit showing as Pending cannot be spent. Bank transfers can take 1–2 business days; on-chain crypto deposits need their confirmations.

3. Below the minimum

The order was under the asset's minimum trade amount. Increase the amount or use Sell all.

4. A limit was hit

You may have reached a daily or monthly transaction limit, or your verification tier's cap. Check Profile → Limits.

5. The quote expired

Quotes last only seconds. If you left the screen open, the confirm will fail. Refresh and try again.

6. The market is suspended

Assets can be paused during network upgrades, listing reviews or extreme volatility. The asset page shows a banner when this is the case. There is nothing to fix — wait for it to reopen.

Still stuck?

  • Force-close and reopen the Betabase app, or hard-refresh www.betabase.pro. Disable any VPN or ad blocker, which can break the order API.
  • Check the Status page at www.betabase.pro for a live incident.
  • If the order shows Rejected, open its detail — the exact reason is printed there. Include that reason and the order ID when you contact support.

Never re-submit a failed order repeatedly. If the first attempt actually went through, you can end up with a duplicate position. Check Transactions first.
