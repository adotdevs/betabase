How to buy cryptocurrency

You buy crypto by opening Trade → Buy, selecting an asset, entering an amount of EUR to spend, reviewing the quote and confirming the order.

Before you start

  • Verification must be complete and you need a settled cash balance. Pending deposits cannot be spent.
  • Check the minimum trade amount for that asset (see Minimum trade amounts).

Steps

  1. From the dashboard, select Trade (or Buy in the Betabase app).
  2. Search for the asset and select it.
  3. Select the Buy tab.
  4. Enter the amount you want to spend in EUR. You can also toggle the input to enter a quantity of the asset instead. Fractions are fine — you never need to buy a whole coin.
  5. Choose your order type: Market (executes now at the best available price) or Limit (executes only at your price or better).
  6. Review the summary: estimated quantity received, price, spread and fee.
  7. Select Confirm. The order appears immediately under Transactions.

Good to know

  • Market orders fill in seconds. Limit orders sit open until the market reaches your price, or until you cancel them.
  • The quote you see is indicative and time-limited. If it expires, refresh it — the market has moved.
  • Your cash is reserved the moment you place a limit order, so it cannot be spent twice.

Crypto is volatile and your capital is at risk. You may get back less than you put in. Nothing here is investment advice, and Betabase never recommends specific assets.
