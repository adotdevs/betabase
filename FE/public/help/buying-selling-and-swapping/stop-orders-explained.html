Stop orders explained

A stop order is an instruction that stays dormant until the market reaches a trigger price, at which point it becomes a live market or limit order.

The two variants

  • Stop-market (often called stop-loss). When the trigger price is hit, a market order is submitted. It will almost certainly fill — but at whatever price is available at that moment, which can be well below your trigger in a fast fall.
  • Stop-limit. When the trigger price is hit, a limit order is submitted at a price you also specify. You control the worst price you accept — but if the market races past it, the order does not fill at all and you keep the position.

Steps

  1. Open the asset and select Sell (or Buy).
  2. Change the order type to Stop or Stop-limit.
  3. Enter the trigger price. For a stop-limit, also enter the limit price.
  4. Enter the amount, review the summary and select Confirm.
  5. The order appears under Open orders with the status Pending trigger.

Good to know

  • Your assets are reserved while the stop is open.
  • Triggers use Betabase's reference price, not a price from another exchange.
  • Stops do not run when an asset is suspended or a market is halted.

A stop order does not guarantee a price or protect you from loss. In a sharp move, a stop-market can fill far below your trigger and a stop-limit may not fill at all. Your capital is at risk.
