Tax Security Deposit – Frequently Asked Questions

A Tax Security Deposit is a temporary, separate security amount used only to complete verification before funds can be released. It is not a tax, not a fee, and not a service charge.

Betabase is not a tax authority and does not independently determine a client’s personal tax liability. This article is general information, not tax advice. Any tax liability is determined in accordance with applicable law and the client’s individual circumstances.

When may tax requirements affect the release of funds?

The release of funds may be subject to applicable tax, regulatory, compliance, and verification requirements relating to the transaction or the client, depending on the applicable law and circumstances.

Where a temporary security mechanism is required to complete the relevant verification process, the client may be required to provide a Tax Security Deposit in accordance with the applicable procedure and the terms communicated to the client.

Important: The Company is not a tax authority and does not independently determine a client’s personal tax liability. Any tax liability is determined in accordance with applicable law and the client’s individual circumstances.

What is a Tax Security Deposit?

A Tax Security Deposit is a temporary security deposit intended to secure the completion of the required verification and review process before the release of funds. The deposit is held temporarily for this purpose only.

The Tax Security Deposit is not a tax payment, is not a fee, is not a service charge, and does not constitute revenue of the Company.

Is the Tax Security Deposit a tax payment?

No. The Tax Security Deposit does not constitute a payment of tax to a tax authority and does not replace or settle any tax liability that may apply to the client.

Where a client has a tax liability under applicable law, that liability must be addressed in accordance with the requirements of the relevant law and competent tax authority. The Tax Security Deposit is a separate security mechanism used solely for the completion of the applicable verification procedure.

Why can’t the Tax Security Deposit simply be deducted from the funds already held in my account?

The Tax Security Deposit is required as a separate security amount from the existing account balance. Depending on the applicable regulatory, contractual, and administrative requirements governing the handling of client funds, the deposit cannot be automatically offset against or deducted from the balance designated for release.

Accordingly, where a security deposit is required, it must be provided separately from the existing funds, in accordance with the applicable procedure communicated to the client. The existing balance does not become the security deposit and cannot be used as a substitute for the required security amount.

How long is the Tax Security Deposit held?

The Tax Security Deposit is held on a temporary basis only. The security period is up to 7 business days, subject to completion of the required verification and review procedures. Where the applicable process is completed earlier, the procedure may be finalized before the end of the seven-business-day period.

Will the Tax Security Deposit be refunded?

Yes. Once the required verification process has been completed and the applicable release conditions have been satisfied, the Tax Security Deposit will be released and returned to the client in full, in accordance with the applicable procedure.

The deposit is not intended to constitute income, a fee, or a payment to the Company.

Can the Company use the Tax Security Deposit to pay my taxes?

No. The Tax Security Deposit does not constitute a tax payment and is not intended to be used to pay a tax authority on behalf of the client, unless an express legal requirement and an applicable mechanism provide otherwise.

The client remains responsible for any personal tax obligations applicable to them. The Company does not act as the client’s personal tax adviser.

What happens after the Tax Security Deposit is provided?

Once the deposit has been received, the Company may proceed with the required verification and review process. Following successful completion of the verification process, and provided that there is no other legal, regulatory, or operational restriction, the withdrawal or release request may proceed to the next administrative stage in accordance with the Company’s procedures.

The Tax Security Deposit will be handled in accordance with the applicable terms and will be returned in full once the relevant release conditions have been satisfied.

Is there any fee or deduction from the Tax Security Deposit?

No. The Tax Security Deposit is not a fee, processing charge, or service charge. Subject to any mandatory legal requirement that may apply, the amount returned to the client will be 100% of the Tax Security Deposit provided, without deduction.

Does the Tax Security Deposit reduce the amount of funds I am due to receive?

No. The Tax Security Deposit is a separate amount from the existing account balance and from the funds designated for release. Once the applicable procedure has been completed, the Tax Security Deposit and the funds eligible for release will be handled in accordance with the applicable release conditions.

What should I do if I am unsure whether the request is legitimate?

Any request for a Tax Security Deposit should be received through an official Company communication channel and should be capable of being independently verified.

The Company will never ask a client to provide passwords, 2FA codes, private keys, or recovery/seed phrases. If you have any doubt regarding a request, contact the Company through its official support channel before taking any action.

Important information

  • A Tax Security Deposit does not, by itself, constitute a tax assessment or a determination of the client’s personal tax liability.
  • The deposit is a temporary and separate security mechanism. It is not a tax, it is not a fee, and it is not a service charge.
  • Security period: up to 7 business days.
  • Refund: 100% of the Tax Security Deposit, subject to the applicable release conditions.
  • Deduction from existing balance: not permitted under this procedure.
  • Purpose: temporary security and verification only.

Clients are encouraged to obtain independent advice from a qualified tax adviser or other appropriate professional regarding any personal tax obligations that may apply to them.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Any request for recovery words, remote-access software, or payment to a personal account is a scam. Use only official channels such as Betabase support.
