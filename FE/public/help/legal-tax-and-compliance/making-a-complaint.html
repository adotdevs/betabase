Making a complaint

Send your complaint to support@www.betabase.pro with the word "Complaint" in the subject line, and it will be handled under our formal complaints procedure rather than as a normal support ticket.

What to include

  1. Your full name and the email address registered to the account. Do not include your password.
  2. A clear description of what happened, in date order.
  3. The dates, amounts and transaction IDs involved.
  4. Copies of any relevant correspondence or screenshots.
  5. What outcome you are seeking. Complaints that state the desired resolution are resolved considerably faster.

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your complaint in writing, normally within a few business days, and give you a reference number.
  2. Your complaint is investigated by someone independent of the original decision.
  3. We send you a final response in writing, setting out our conclusion, our reasoning, and any redress we are offering.
  4. Our full complaints policy, including our target timescales, is published under Legal → Complaints on www.betabase.pro.

If you're still not satisfied

  • You may be able to refer the matter to an independent ombudsman or alternative dispute resolution scheme, free of charge. Eligibility depends on your country and the nature of the complaint.
  • You may also raise the matter with the relevant national financial authority, and — for a data-protection matter — with your national data-protection authority.
  • Your final response letter will tell you which routes are open to you and by when you must use them.

Beware of "claims management" or "fund recovery" firms that contact you after a loss and ask for an upfront fee. Complaining directly is free.
