Data protection and how we use your personal data

We collect the personal data we need to run your account, meet our legal obligations and keep you safe — and no more. The Privacy Policy on www.betabase.pro is the full, binding statement.

What we collect and why

  • Identity and contact data (name, date of birth, address, ID document, email, phone) — to open your account and meet KYC/AML obligations.
  • Financial data (transactions, balances, bank details) — to provide the service and produce your records.
  • Technical data (device, IP address, session logs) — to secure your account and detect fraud.

Who we share it with

Regulated service providers acting for us (identity verification, payment processing, cloud hosting), and regulators, tax authorities and law enforcement where we are legally required to disclose. We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access the data we hold, to have inaccurate data corrected, to request erasure, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to receive your data in a portable format. You can also withdraw marketing consent at any time.

Steps to exercise a right

  1. Go to Profile → Settings → Privacy and use the self-service tools where available.
  2. Otherwise email support@www.betabase.pro with your request. We must verify your identity before we act.
  3. We respond within the statutory deadline that applies in your jurisdiction.

Good to know

  • Erasure is not absolute. AML law requires us to retain identity and transaction records for a set minimum period after your account closes. We cannot delete those on request.
  • If you are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your national data-protection authority.
