AML and KYC: what we're required to do

AML (anti-money laundering) and KYC (know your customer) are the legal obligations that require us to verify who you are, screen you against sanctions lists, and monitor activity for signs of financial crime.

What we are required to do

  • Verify your identity before you can deposit or trade — a government-issued photo ID and, where needed, proof of address.
  • Screen every customer against sanctions lists, and identify politically exposed persons (PEPs), on registration and continuously thereafter.
  • Understand your activity — the purpose of your account and, where relevant, your source of funds and source of wealth.
  • Monitor transactions on an ongoing basis for unusual or suspicious patterns.
  • Keep records for a minimum period set by law, even after your account is closed.
  • Report suspicious activity to the relevant authority where required.

What this means for you in practice

  • You cannot use Betabase anonymously, and you cannot use it on behalf of someone else. Deposits must come from an account in your own name.
  • We may re-verify you periodically, or ask for updated documents, even if you verified years ago.
  • We may restrict, freeze or close an account where we cannot meet these obligations.

Good to know

  • These rules apply to every regulated firm. They are not specific to Betabase and they are not optional for us.
  • Where the law requires it, we may be prohibited from telling you that a report has been made or why an account has been restricted. This is a legal constraint on us, not evasion.
  • Providing false information is grounds for immediate closure and may be reported to the authorities.
