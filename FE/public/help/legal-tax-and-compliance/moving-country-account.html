What happens to my account if I move country

Update your address in Profile → Personal details as soon as you move. Your access depends on your country of residence, so this can change what you're able to do.

Steps

  1. Go to Profile → Personal details → Residential address.
  2. Enter your new address and submit.
  3. Upload a new proof of address if asked — a utility bill, bank statement or official letter dated within the last 3 months.
  4. Complete any re-verification prompts. Moving country routinely triggers a fresh compliance review.
  5. Check Legal → Supported countries to see what is available where you now live.

What could change

  • Product availability. Some features — staking, savings plans, card products, certain assets — are restricted by country even where the account itself works normally.
  • Payment methods. Local payment rails may differ, and your existing bank account may no longer be usable.
  • Tax. Your tax residence may change, which may change your obligations. This depends entirely on your jurisdiction and your circumstances — consult a qualified tax adviser before and after you move.
  • Reporting. We may be required to report to a different tax authority.

If you move to a country we do not serve

Your account moves to withdrawal-only: you can sell your holdings and withdraw your funds, but you cannot deposit or buy. You'll be given a reasonable period to close your position.

Do not keep an out-of-date address on file, and never use a VPN to disguise where you live. Both breach the Terms of Service and can lead to your account being frozen.
