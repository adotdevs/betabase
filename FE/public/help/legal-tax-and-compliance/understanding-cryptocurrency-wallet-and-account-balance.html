Understanding your cryptocurrency wallet and account balance

How we manage customer cryptocurrency balances, why a deposit address is not always a wallet exclusive to you, and how your account balance is recorded.

How we manage customer cryptocurrency balances

At Betabase, we use a custodial wallet infrastructure to securely manage cryptocurrency assets and customer balances.

When you deposit cryptocurrency, the blockchain address displayed for your deposit does not necessarily represent a separate blockchain wallet owned exclusively by you. Instead, the deposit address is part of our wallet infrastructure, while your individual cryptocurrency balance is recorded and managed within your exchange account.

This structure allows us to securely and efficiently manage customer assets while maintaining an accurate record of each customer's individual holdings.

How your balance is recorded

When you make a cryptocurrency deposit, the transaction is recorded on the relevant blockchain. Once the required confirmations have been completed, we credit the corresponding amount to your exchange account.

Your individual balance is maintained through our internal accounting and ledger system, which records the assets allocated to your account.

The underlying blockchain assets may be held together with assets belonging to other customers within our custodial wallet infrastructure. However, your account balance remains individually recorded within our system.

The three key components

Blockchain address

The blockchain address used to receive or send cryptocurrency. Transactions involving this address can be verified on the relevant blockchain.

Your exchange account

Your personal account on our platform, where your cryptocurrency balances, transaction history, and account activity are recorded.

Our internal ledger

Our internal accounting system that records and maintains the cryptocurrency balance allocated to your exchange account.

Why you may not have a permanently dedicated blockchain wallet

As a large-scale cryptocurrency platform, we use custodial infrastructure to manage blockchain assets efficiently.

It is therefore possible for multiple customers' assets to be held within the same underlying wallet infrastructure, while each customer's balance remains separately recorded and accounted for within our internal systems.

For this reason, you may see allocated balances, sub-accounts, or internally separated account records rather than a blockchain wallet that is permanently and exclusively assigned to you.

Does this mean my funds are not allocated to my account?

No.

The absence of a permanently dedicated blockchain address does not mean that your cryptocurrency balance is missing or that it is not associated with your account.

Your balance is linked to your exchange account through our internal accounting system. Your deposits, withdrawals, transactions, and current balance are recorded against your individual account.

The blockchain provides the public record of transactions, while our internal ledger provides the customer-level accounting required to manage balances within our platform.

Custodial asset management

Our model is commonly referred to as custodial asset management.

Under this model, we maintain custody of the underlying blockchain assets and manage them through our wallet infrastructure, while providing each customer with an individually recorded balance within their exchange account.

This approach is widely used by large cryptocurrency exchanges because it allows us to manage customer assets efficiently while maintaining individual account records.

In simple terms

  • Your blockchain address identifies where cryptocurrency is sent on the blockchain.
  • Your exchange account identifies you within our platform.
  • Our internal ledger records how much cryptocurrency is allocated to your account.

These systems work together to ensure that your cryptocurrency balance is properly recorded and associated with your account, even when the underlying blockchain assets are managed through shared custodial wallet infrastructure.

Important: Blockchain wallet addresses and exchange account balances are separate concepts. The fact that your account does not have a permanently dedicated blockchain wallet address does not, by itself, indicate an issue with your account or balance.
