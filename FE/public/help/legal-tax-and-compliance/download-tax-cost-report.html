How to download a tax / cost report

Go to Transactions → Export → Tax & cost report, choose the period, and download the file for your adviser or your tax software.

Steps

  1. Open Transactions on the dashboard.
  2. Select Export, then choose Tax & cost report (rather than a plain statement).
  3. Set the period to your jurisdiction's tax year — check the exact start and end dates, as they are not 1 January to 31 December everywhere.
  4. Include all transaction types: buys, sells, swaps, transfers in and out, fees, rewards and savings-plan executions. Omitting any of these will make the report incomplete.
  5. Choose CSV for tax software, or PDF for a readable summary.
  6. Select Generate. Large reports are prepared in the background and emailed to you.
  7. Save the file, and keep it for as long as your jurisdiction requires you to retain records.

Good to know

  • The report is generated in your settlement currency, not your display currency.
  • The report covers your activity on Betabase only. If you have used other platforms, held self-custody wallets, or transferred assets in from outside, your adviser will need those records too, or the cost basis will be wrong.
  • Cost-basis methods (FIFO, average cost and others) differ by jurisdiction. Confirm which one applies to you before relying on any figure.

This report is a record of your transactions. It is not a tax return, it is not tax advice, and it does not tell you what you owe. Tax treatment depends on your jurisdiction and circumstances — have it reviewed by a qualified tax adviser.
