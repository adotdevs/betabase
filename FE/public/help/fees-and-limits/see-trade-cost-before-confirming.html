How to see exactly what a trade will cost before you confirm

Every order screen shows a summary before you commit. Read it, and you know the full cost — there is nothing added afterwards.

Steps

  1. Build the order as normal: pick the asset, the side (buy or sell) and the amount.
  2. Select Preview / Review order. The summary appears.
  3. Read these four lines:

- You pay — the total leaving your balance, fee included. - You receive — the estimated quantity of the asset (or of EUR on a sell). - Price — the rate used. Compare it with the mid-market price you saw on the chart; the gap is the spread. - Fee — the explicit commission, if your market charges one.

  1. Note the quote timer. If it runs out, the quote refreshes and the numbers change.
  2. Select Confirm while the quote is live.

The best single check

Ignore the fee percentage and look at You receive. That number already contains the spread, the commission and everything else. Change the amount and watch it move — this is the only figure that tells you the real cost.

Good to know

  • On a market order, You receive is an estimate. The final figure can differ slightly because of slippage.
  • On a limit order, your price is guaranteed if it fills at all — but the fill is not.
  • The final, exact figures are on the transaction record under Transactions once the order settles.
