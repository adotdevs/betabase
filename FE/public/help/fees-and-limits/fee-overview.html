Betabase fee overview

Betabase charges in four places: on trades, on some deposits, on some withdrawals, and on network transfers. Everything is published on the Fees & limits page at www.betabase.pro, and shown to you before you confirm.

The four categories

  • Trading fees. Charged when an order executes. Depending on your region and asset, this is a spread built into the price, an explicit commission, or both. Nothing is charged on an order that does not fill.
  • Deposit fees. Standard bank transfers are commonly free. Cards and some instant methods carry a percentage fee because the payment provider charges one.
  • Withdrawal fees. EUR withdrawals to your bank may carry a flat fee. Crypto withdrawals carry a network fee, which is paid to the blockchain, not to Betabase.
  • Other fees. Some regions apply an inactivity fee after a long dormant period, or fees for premium products. These are always listed in the fee schedule.

Good to know

  • The full cost of any transaction is shown in the confirmation summary before you commit. Betabase does not add charges after execution.
  • Fees can differ by country, asset, payment method and verification tier. The fee schedule is the authoritative source; this article is a summary.
  • Fees may change. Material changes are announced in advance in line with the Terms of Service.
  • Third parties may charge you separately — your bank, your card issuer (which may treat a crypto purchase as a cash advance) or a foreign-exchange conversion.
