Deposit fees by method

Deposit fees depend entirely on the method you choose. Bank transfers are usually the cheapest route; cards are usually the most expensive.

By method

  • Standard bank transfer. Commonly free, or a small flat fee. Takes 1–2 business days and does not move at weekends or on bank holidays. The cheapest option for larger amounts.
  • Instant bank payment / open banking. Often free or a low flat fee, and credits within minutes. Availability depends on your bank and country.
  • Debit or credit card. Convenient and instant, but carries a percentage fee — the card networks charge Betabase and the cost is passed on. Typically the most expensive method.
  • Crypto deposit. Betabase does not charge to receive crypto. You pay the network fee on the sending side, which is set by the blockchain, not by Betabase.

Good to know

  • The exact fee is shown on the deposit screen before you commit, and the minimum deposit is €10.
  • Your own bank or card issuer may add its own charge. Some issuers treat a crypto purchase as a cash advance, which can carry a higher rate and immediate interest — check with them.
  • Depositing in a currency other than EUR can trigger a foreign-exchange conversion, either by Betabase or by your bank. That conversion has a cost even when the deposit fee is zero.
  • Third-party payments are rejected and returned. The refund can take days and may cost a fee.
