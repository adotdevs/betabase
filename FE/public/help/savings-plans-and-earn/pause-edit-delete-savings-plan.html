How to pause, edit or delete a savings plan

You can pause, edit or delete any plan at any time from Savings plans. Changes take effect from the next execution, never retroactively.

Steps

  1. Open Savings plans and select the plan you want to change.
  2. Select Manage, then choose:

- Pause — the plan stops running but keeps all its settings. Resume it whenever you like. - Edit — change the amount, frequency, execution day, asset split or funding source. - Delete — remove the plan permanently.

  1. Confirm. The plan's status and next execution date update immediately.

Good to know

  • Your existing crypto is not affected. Pausing or deleting a plan stops future buys only. Everything already purchased stays in your portfolio until you choose to sell it.
  • Changes made very close to an execution time may not apply to that run — the instalment may already be in flight. Cancel at least a day ahead to be certain.
  • Pausing does not cancel a direct debit at your bank; it simply stops Betabase collecting. If you want the mandate gone entirely, delete the plan.
  • There is no fee to pause, edit or delete a plan.
  • A deleted plan cannot be restored. Its history remains under Transactions, but you would need to create a new plan from scratch.

Do not cancel a direct debit at your bank without first pausing or deleting the plan on Betabase. A failed collection can leave your account short and may incur a charge from your bank.
