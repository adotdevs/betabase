How to create a savings plan

Create a plan from Savings plans → Create plan: pick the asset, the amount, how often it should run, and where the money comes from.

Before you start

  • Verification must be complete.
  • Decide how the plan is funded: from your existing cash balance on Betabase, or by an automatic direct debit / card charge where your country supports it.
  • Only commit an amount you can genuinely afford to lose, every single month, indefinitely.

Steps

  1. Open Savings plans from the dashboard, then select Create plan.
  2. Choose the asset. You can add more than one asset to a single plan and split the instalment by percentage, where supported.
  3. Enter the instalment amount in EUR. The minimum is shown under the field.
  4. Choose the frequency: weekly, fortnightly or monthly.
  5. Choose the start date and, for monthly plans, the day of the month. If that day doesn't exist in a given month, the plan runs on the last day.
  6. Choose the funding source.
  7. Review the summary — asset split, amount, frequency, fee — and select Create plan.
  8. The plan appears under Savings plans with its next execution date.

Good to know

  • Each execution is a market order and carries the normal spread and fee.
  • The first instalment runs on your chosen start date, not immediately.
  • You receive a confirmation after every execution under Transactions.

Rewards, prices and outcomes are not guaranteed. Crypto is volatile and your capital is at risk.
