Staking rewards, lock-up periods and unstaking

Rewards accrue while your assets are staked and are paid out on the network's own schedule. Unstaking is not instant — most networks impose an unbonding period before you get your assets back.

Rewards

  • Rewards are paid in the staked asset, not in EUR.
  • The rate is variable and set by the network, not by Betabase. It changes as network participation changes. Any figure shown in the app is an estimate based on recent conditions, not a promise.
  • Payout frequency varies by network — daily, per epoch, or per era. Some networks have a warm-up period before the first reward arrives.
  • Any service fee is deducted before the reward is credited and is disclosed in the fee schedule.

Lock-up and unstaking

  1. Select the staked asset and choose Unstake.
  2. Enter the amount and confirm.
  3. The asset enters an unbonding period set by the network — this can range from a few hours to several weeks depending on the chain.
  4. During unbonding you earn no rewards and cannot sell, swap or withdraw the asset.
  5. When the period ends, the balance returns to your available portfolio automatically.

Good to know

  • Betabase cannot shorten or waive an unbonding period. It is a rule of the blockchain.
  • The price can move a long way while you are locked up. This is the central risk of staking.

Your capital is at risk. You cannot exit a staked position during the lock-up or unbonding period, even in a sharp market fall. Rewards are variable, may be reduced, and are never guaranteed.
