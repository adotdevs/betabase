I cannot log in to my account (troubleshooting)

Work through the cause: wrong credentials, a 2FA code problem, an undelivered email or SMS, or a temporary lock after too many failed attempts.

1. "Invalid email or password"

  • Check for a stray space, a capital letter, or the wrong keyboard layout.
  • Make sure you are using the email you actually registered with — try any alternatives you own.
  • Still stuck? Use Forgot password? to reset.

2. Your 2FA code is rejected

  • Codes rotate every 30 seconds. Wait for a fresh one and type it quickly.
  • Set your phone's date and time to automatic. A clock that has drifted is by far the most common cause of "invalid code".
  • Confirm you are reading the code for the right account inside your authenticator app.
  • No device? See How to reset 2FA when you lose your device.

3. No email or SMS arriving

  • Check spam and promotions folders; whitelist our sending domain.
  • SMS: check signal, disable any call/SMS blocker, and note that roaming can block delivery.
  • Wait 5 minutes before requesting a resend — rapid resends can queue behind each other.

4. "Too many attempts" / temporarily locked

  • Failed logins trigger a temporary lock. Wait 30 minutes and try again — do not keep retrying, that extends the lock.

Good to know

  • If none of this works, or you see activity you do not recognise, freeze the account and contact support@www.betabase.pro. Support is available 24/7.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Support will never ask you to log in on their behalf.
