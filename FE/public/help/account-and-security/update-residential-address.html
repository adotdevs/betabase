How to update your residential address

Update your address in Settings → Personal details → Address. Most changes require a fresh proof-of-address document.

Before you start

Prepare a document dated within the last 3 months showing your full name and the new address:

  • a utility bill (gas, electricity, water, landline — not mobile);
  • a bank or credit-card statement;
  • a council tax, tax authority or other government letter;
  • a residence certificate.

The file must be a full-page, unedited colour scan or photo. All four corners must be visible.

Steps

  1. Open Profile → Settings → Personal details.
  2. Next to Address, select Change.
  3. Enter the new address exactly as it appears on your document.
  4. Upload your proof of address.
  5. Select Submit. The status shows In review.

Good to know

  • Review usually completes within 1–3 business days. You get an email either way.
  • Documents are rejected if they are cropped, blurred, expired, screenshotted from a banking app without the name and date visible, or in a different name.
  • If you have moved to a country Betabase does not serve, your account will move to withdrawal-only. You will be able to sell and withdraw, but not deposit or buy.
  • Keeping your address current is a legal requirement, not an optional setting.
