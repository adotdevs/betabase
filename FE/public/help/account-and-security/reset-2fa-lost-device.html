How to reset 2FA when you lose your device

If you still have a backup code, use it to sign in and re-pair 2FA yourself. If you have no backup codes, request a 2FA reset — this requires an identity check.

Option 1 — you have a backup code (fastest)

  1. On the login screen, enter your email and password.
  2. At the 2FA prompt, select Use a backup code.
  3. Enter one unused backup code. Each code works once.
  4. Once in, go to Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication → Reset and pair your new device.
  5. Generate a fresh set of backup codes — the old set is now void.

Option 2 — no backup codes (identity check required)

  1. On the login screen, select Can't access your 2FA? — or open the Reset 2FA tool at www.betabase.pro.
  2. Confirm your email address. We send you a link.
  3. Complete a liveness check: a short video selfie plus your photo ID, so we can confirm you are the account holder.
  4. Submit the request and wait. This is a manual review.

Good to know

  • A 2FA reset typically takes 1–3 business days. It cannot be rushed — the delay is the security.
  • Withdrawals are locked for a cooling-off period (commonly 24–72 hours) after a 2FA reset. This is deliberate and protects you if an attacker were attempting the reset.
  • Your funds are safe while you wait. Nobody can withdraw without the new 2FA.
  • Support cannot bypass 2FA, no matter how urgent the situation is.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. "Fast 2FA reset" services on social media are scams, without exception.
