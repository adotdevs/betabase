What are backup codes and how to use them

Backup codes are one-time codes that let you log in when your 2FA device is unavailable. You get them when you switch 2FA on — save them then.

How to use one

  1. Sign in with your email and password.
  2. At the 2FA prompt, select Use a backup code.
  3. Enter one unused code. It is consumed immediately and cannot be reused.
  4. Once inside, go to Settings → Security and re-pair 2FA on your new device.

Where to find or regenerate them

  1. Open Profile → Settings → Security → Two-factor authentication.
  2. Select Backup codes → Generate new codes.
  3. Confirm with your password and a current 2FA code.
  4. Store the new set. Generating a new set invalidates every previous code.

How to store them

  • Print them, or write them out, and keep them somewhere physically secure — a safe, a locked drawer, with important documents.
  • Or store them in a reputable password manager.
  • Do not keep them in your phone's photo gallery, in a plain text file on your desktop, in your email, or in cloud notes. Those are exactly the places an attacker looks first.

Good to know

  • Each code works once. When you have used most of them, regenerate the set.
  • Backup codes are the only way to get back in without a manual 2FA reset, which takes days.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Anyone who has your backup codes can take your account — treat them like cash.
