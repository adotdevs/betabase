How to freeze (lock) your account

Freezing immediately blocks all logins, trades and withdrawals on your account. Use it the moment you suspect something is wrong.

When to freeze

  • You see a login, trade or withdrawal you did not make.
  • You clicked a suspicious link and entered your details.
  • Your phone or laptop has been lost or stolen.
  • You gave someone remote access to your device.

Steps

  1. If you can sign in: go to Profile → Settings → Security → Freeze account, confirm, and select Freeze now.
  2. If you cannot sign in: open the Freeze my account self-service tool at www.betabase.pro (no login required) and confirm via the link sent to your registered email.
  3. Email support@www.betabase.pro from your registered address as a backup route, with the subject line Account compromise — freeze.

Good to know

  • A freeze takes effect immediately and applies to web and app at once.
  • While frozen: no logins, no trades, no deposits, no withdrawals. Your balances stay exactly as they are.
  • Open orders may be cancelled. Market prices continue to move while frozen — Betabase cannot compensate for price movement during a freeze.
  • Freezing is reversible. See How to reactivate a frozen account.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. After freezing, change your email password too — that is usually the account the attacker really wants.
