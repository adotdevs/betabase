Authenticator app vs SMS 2FA

An authenticator app is significantly more secure than SMS, because SMS codes can be intercepted through SIM-swap fraud. Use an app wherever you can.

How they compare

Authenticator appSMS
Works without signalYesNo
Works abroad / roamingYesOften unreliable
Vulnerable to SIM swapNoYes
Vulnerable to carrier delaysNoYes
Recoverable if you lose the phoneOnly with backup codesOnly with the number

What a SIM swap is

An attacker persuades your mobile carrier to move your number to a SIM they control — usually with details harvested from a data breach or from you directly. Every SMS code then goes to them. They do not need your phone. This is the single most common way crypto accounts with SMS 2FA are taken over.

Good to know

  • Set a port-out PIN / account passcode with your mobile carrier if you must use SMS.
  • An authenticator app generates codes locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted, so nothing can be intercepted.
  • Whichever you use, back it up: save your backup codes and consider pairing a second device.
  • Hardware security keys, where supported, are stronger still.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. No genuine employee will ever ask you to read a code aloud.
