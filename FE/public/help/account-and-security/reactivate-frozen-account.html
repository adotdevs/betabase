How to reactivate a frozen account

Reactivate through the Reactivate account tool at www.betabase.pro or in Settings → Security. If the freeze followed a compromise, expect an identity check first.

Before you start

Clean up first, or you will simply be re-compromised:

  • Change your Betabase password and your email password.
  • Re-pair 2FA on a device you trust and generate fresh backup codes.
  • Run a malware scan on any device you signed in from.
  • Remove any browser extension or remote-access app you did not install yourself.

Steps

  1. Go to www.betabase.pro and open the Reactivate account tool, or sign in and open Settings → Security → Reactivate.
  2. Confirm your identity — normally an email link plus a 2FA code, or a liveness check if the freeze was triggered by suspected fraud.
  3. Review the list of active sessions and payout addresses and revoke anything you do not recognise.
  4. Confirm reactivation.

Good to know

  • Self-service freezes usually lift within minutes once identity is confirmed.
  • Freezes applied by Betabase for compliance or fraud reasons are lifted only after review, which can take several business days.
  • Withdrawals stay locked for a cooling-off period (commonly 24–72 hours) after reactivation. Trading normally resumes at once.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Nobody can reactivate your account "for a fee" — those offers are scams.
