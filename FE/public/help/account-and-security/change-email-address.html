How to change your registered email address

Change your email in Settings → Personal details → Email address. You confirm the change from both your old and your new inbox.

Before you start

  • You need access to your current email address. If you have lost it, contact support@www.betabase.pro — this requires manual identity checks.
  • Have your 2FA device to hand.

Steps

  1. Open Profile → Settings → Personal details.
  2. Next to Email address, select Change.
  3. Enter your password and your 2FA code.
  4. Enter the new email address and select Continue.
  5. Open the verification link sent to your old address to authorise the change.
  6. Open the verification link sent to your new address to activate it.
  7. The change takes effect once both links are confirmed.

Good to know

  • Both links expire. If one lapses, restart the process.
  • Your login username is your email, so sign in with the new address from then on.
  • For your protection, withdrawals are typically paused for up to 24 hours after an email change.
  • The new address cannot already be in use on another Betabase account.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. If you get an email saying your address was changed and it wasn't you, act immediately: freeze your account and contact support.
