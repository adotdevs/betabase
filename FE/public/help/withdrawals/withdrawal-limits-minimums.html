Withdrawal limits and minimums

The minimum withdrawal is €20. Maximums depend on your verification level, the asset and your country — your exact figures are always under Profile → Limits.

Steps to check and raise your limits

  1. Go to Profile → Limits.
  2. Read the row for the withdrawal type you want — the per-transaction cap and the rolling daily and 30-day caps are listed separately.
  3. To raise them, select Increase limits and complete the next verification tier. This may require proof of address, a tax number, or source-of-funds evidence.

Good to know

  • Limits are rolling, not calendar-based. A 24-hour limit refreshes 24 hours after each withdrawal, not at midnight.
  • Crypto minimums are set per asset, because a small amount can be worth less than the network fee needed to move it.
  • Fiat and crypto limits are tracked separately.
  • Very large withdrawals may be routed to a compliance review even when they are inside your limit. This is normal and is not a block.
  • Newly added bank accounts and wallet addresses can have a temporary lower cap for the first few days.

If you need more than your limit

  • Complete the next verification tier — this is the fastest route.
  • Or split the withdrawal across several days, staying inside the rolling window each time.
  • For a genuinely exceptional one-off amount, contact support@www.betabase.pro before you start. Support is available 24/7.
