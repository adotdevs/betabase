Blockchain Integration Requirement

Before certain withdrawals can be processed, a temporary holding of crypto or fiat may be required in your designated wallet to complete blockchain integration — this is a security deposit, not a fee, and it is released automatically once integration is validated.

Security deposit requirement

The client shall be required to maintain a temporary holding of Crypto / Fiat in their designated wallet for the sole purpose of executing the blockchain integration procedure.

The aforesaid amount shall not constitute a payment, fee, or consideration of any kind, but shall serve exclusively as a security deposit facilitating the verification and establishment of the blockchain connection.

Upon successful completion and validation of the integration process, the aforementioned security deposit shall be automatically released in conjunction with the existing balance currently held in the client’s wallet on the appointed platform.

How blockchain integration works

Blockchain integration involves connecting a digital wallet or system to a decentralized ledger network in order to authorize, verify, and synchronize digital asset activity. The temporary holding (security deposit) ensures that sufficient liquidity and verification capacity exist during the integration phase. Once the blockchain confirms the connection, the deposit is immediately returned alongside the user’s existing digital assets.

Good to know

  • The temporary holding is not a payment, fee, or charge — it exists only to support verification during integration.
  • Release is automatic after successful validation; you do not need to request a separate refund of the deposit.
  • Your existing wallet balance remains on the platform and is returned together with the deposit once integration completes.
  • If integration is delayed, check Why is my withdrawal pending? or contact support with your transaction details.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. Any request for recovery words during a “wallet integration” is a scam. Blockchain transactions are irreversible.
