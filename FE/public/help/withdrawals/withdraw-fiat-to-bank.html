How to withdraw EUR to your bank account

Sell your crypto for EUR first, then open Withdraw, choose your own bank account, enter an amount and confirm with 2FA.

Before you start

  • You need a settled cash balance — crypto must be sold before it can be withdrawn as EUR.
  • The destination bank account must be in your own name. Third-party withdrawals are blocked.
  • The minimum withdrawal is €20.

Steps

  1. From the dashboard, select Withdraw, then EUR.
  2. Choose a saved bank account, or select Add bank account and enter the IBAN/account number and BIC/SWIFT. The account holder name must match your verified name exactly.
  3. Enter the amount. Your available balance and any fee are shown.
  4. Review the summary: amount, fee, and the net amount that will reach your bank.
  5. Select Confirm and complete your 2FA challenge.
  6. The withdrawal appears in Transactions as Pending while it is processed.

Good to know

  • SEPA withdrawals typically arrive in 1–2 business days. International wires take 2–5 business days and intermediary banks may deduct a fee.
  • Banks do not process at weekends or on public holidays.
  • A newly added bank account may trigger a short security hold the first time you use it.
  • Funds from a very recent card deposit may be held before they can be withdrawn, to guard against payment fraud.

Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys. If someone asks you to withdraw funds and forward them, you are being scammed.
