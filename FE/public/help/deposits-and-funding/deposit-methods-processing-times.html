Supported deposit methods and processing times

Betabase supports bank transfers, instant bank payments, debit/credit cards and crypto transfers — speeds range from seconds to several business days.

The methods

MethodTypical speedNotes
Instant bank paymentSeconds to minutesApproved directly in your banking app. Availability depends on your bank.
SEPA transfer1–2 business daysEuro area. SEPA Instant, where supported, arrives in seconds.
International wire (SWIFT)2–5 business daysIntermediary bank fees may be deducted en route.
Debit / credit cardMinutesHigher fee. Credit cards are often blocked by the issuer.
Crypto transferMinutes to ~1 hourDepends on the network and the number of confirmations required.

Good to know

  • Availability varies by country. The Deposit screen only ever shows the methods you can actually use.
  • Bank rails do not run at weekends or on public holidays. Crypto networks run 24/7, including weekends.
  • The clock starts when your bank sends the money, not when you press the button in their app — some banks queue payments.
  • Fees differ sharply between methods. The total, including fee, is always shown before you confirm.
  • A first deposit with a new method may take slightly longer while additional checks run.
