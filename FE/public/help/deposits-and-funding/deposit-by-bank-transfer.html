How to deposit EUR by bank transfer (SEPA/wire)

Deposit by bank transfer by copying the account details and reference code shown in your account, then making the payment from your own banking app.

Before you start

  • Identity verification must be complete.
  • The sending bank account must be in your own name. Third-party payments are rejected and returned.
  • Have your banking app or online banking open.

Steps

  1. On the dashboard, select Deposit, then choose EUR.
  2. Select Bank transfer.
  3. Enter the amount you intend to send. The minimum is €10.
  4. Betabase shows you the beneficiary details: account name, IBAN/account number, BIC/SWIFT and a unique reference code.
  5. Open your banking app and set up a payment to exactly those details.
  6. Paste the reference code into the payment reference / description field. Copy it character for character — do not add words, do not translate it, do not put your own name there instead.
  7. Send the payment and return to Betabase. The deposit appears under Transactions as Pending once we detect it.

Good to know

  • SEPA transfers within the euro area typically credit in 1–2 business days. SEPA Instant, where your bank supports it, can arrive in seconds.
  • International wires (SWIFT) typically take 2–5 business days and your bank may charge its own fee, plus intermediary banks may deduct a cut.
  • Banks do not process transfers at weekends or on public holidays. A Friday-afternoon payment usually lands on Monday or Tuesday.
  • Your beneficiary details may differ from another user's. Always copy them fresh from your own logged-in account each time.

Only ever send money to the bank details shown inside your logged-in Betabase account. Details sent to you by email, chat, phone or social media are a scam. Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys.
