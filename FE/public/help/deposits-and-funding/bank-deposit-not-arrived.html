My bank deposit hasn't arrived

Most missing bank deposits are one of four things: it is simply still in transit, the reference code was missing or wrong, the payment came from a third-party account, or the beneficiary details were mistyped.

Steps

  1. Check the clock. Instant payments arrive in minutes; SEPA in 1–2 business days; wires in 2–5. Weekends and public holidays do not count. If you are inside that window, wait.
  2. Check your bank. Confirm the payment actually left your account — some banks queue or hold payments, especially first-time ones to a new beneficiary.
  3. Check the reference code. Compare what you typed against the code shown in Deposit → Bank transfer. A missing, shortened or altered reference is the single most common cause of a delayed deposit — the money reaches us but cannot be matched to your account.
  4. Check the sender name. If the account is not in your name, the payment will be returned rather than credited. See Why must the bank account be in my own name?
  5. Check the amount. Below €10, or above your limit, and the payment may be held or returned.
  6. Check the beneficiary details. Compare the IBAN/account number and BIC against what your account shows.

If it is still missing

Contact support@www.betabase.pro with:

  • The exact amount and currency.
  • The date and time the payment was sent.
  • The sending account holder's name and the last four digits of the account/IBAN.
  • A PDF or screenshot of the bank confirmation / SWIFT MT103 showing the reference field.

Support is available 24/7. Unmatched payments can usually be located and credited manually, but it takes a few business days.

Never respond to a stranger who contacts you offering to "recover" or "release" a delayed deposit. Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys.
