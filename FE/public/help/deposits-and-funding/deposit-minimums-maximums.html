Minimum and maximum deposit amounts

The minimum deposit is €10. Maximums depend on your verification level, your payment method and your country — your exact figures are shown under Profile → Limits.

Steps to check your limits

  1. Go to Profile → Limits.
  2. Read the row for the method you plan to use — the minimum, the per-transaction cap and the rolling daily/monthly cap are shown separately.
  3. If you need more, select Increase limits and complete the next verification tier.

Good to know

  • Sending less than €10 by bank transfer usually means the payment is returned to you, minus any bank fees. Small crypto deposits below the network minimum may be unrecoverable.
  • Sending more than your cap means the payment is held pending review, or returned. Split large amounts across days, or raise your limits first.
  • Limits are usually rolling (a 24-hour or 30-day window), not calendar-based.
  • Crypto deposit minimums are set per asset, because a tiny amount can be worth less than the cost of moving it.
  • New accounts and newly added payment methods sometimes have a temporary lower cap that lifts automatically.

Never send crypto below the stated minimum for that asset. Deposits under the minimum may be lost permanently, and blockchain transactions cannot be reversed.
