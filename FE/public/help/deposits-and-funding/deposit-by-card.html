How to deposit by card

Card deposits credit your account within minutes: add your card, complete the 3-D Secure check with your bank, and the funds appear immediately.

Before you start

  • Verification complete.
  • The card must be in your own name and issued in a country we support.
  • Card deposits usually carry a higher fee than a bank transfer. Check Fees & limits first.

Steps

  1. Select Deposit → EUR → Debit / credit card.
  2. Enter the amount. The minimum is €10.
  3. Add the card: number, expiry, CVC and the billing address registered with your bank.
  4. Review the summary — amount, fee and total charged.
  5. Select Confirm. You are redirected to your bank's 3-D Secure page.
  6. Approve the payment in your banking app, or enter the one-time code your bank sends.
  7. You return to Betabase and the balance updates, usually within a minute.

Good to know

  • Credit card issuers frequently block crypto purchases as a matter of policy, and many treat them as a cash advance with extra interest. A debit card is usually the better choice.
  • If the payment is declined, the decline comes from your bank, not from Betabase. Call the number on the back of your card and ask them to allow the transaction.
  • Prepaid cards, virtual cards and cards in someone else's name are not accepted.
  • Card deposits cannot be reversed by chargeback once crypto has been bought. Raising a fraudulent chargeback will freeze the account.

Only enter card details on www.betabase.pro or in the Betabase app. Betabase will never ask for your password, 2FA code, seed phrase or private keys, and will never ask you to read out a card code over the phone.
